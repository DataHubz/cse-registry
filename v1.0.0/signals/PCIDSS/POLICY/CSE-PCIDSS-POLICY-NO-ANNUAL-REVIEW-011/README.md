# CSE-PCIDSS-POLICY-NO-ANNUAL-REVIEW-011

**No Annual Policy Review**

## Signal Overview

| Field           | Value                                    |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------|
| Identifier      | `CSE-PCIDSS-POLICY-NO-ANNUAL-REVIEW-011` |
| Domain          | PCIDSS                                   |
| Category        | POLICY                                   |
| Status          | Active                                   |
| Introduced In   | 1.0.0                                    |

## Description

Security policies are not reviewed at least annually.

## Applicability

This signal applies to:

- Organizations processing, storing, or transmitting cardholder data
- Security and compliance teams
- Human resources and training departments
- Third-party service providers (TPSPs)
- Incident response teams

## PCI DSS Context (Informative)

**Requirement:** 12.1 - A comprehensive information security policy that governs and provides direction for protection of the entity's information assets is known and current

**Sub-Requirement:** 12.1.1+

**Regulatory Text:** "The information security policy is reviewed at least once every 12 months and updated as needed based on the results of the reviews to reflect changes to business objectives or the risk environment."

These references are informative and do not constitute compliance guidance.

## Notes

Detection guidance: Monitor for absence of annual policy review documentation, including review dates, participants, changes made, and approval signatures. Verify that all security policies and procedures are reviewed annually and updated to reflect environmental or business changes. Check for policy version control and distribution tracking.
