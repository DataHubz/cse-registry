# CSE-FEDRAMP-SR-NO-SCRM-POLICY-001

**No Supply Chain Risk Management Policy**

## Signal Overview

| Field           | Value                                    |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------|
| Identifier      | `CSE-FEDRAMP-SR-NO-SCRM-POLICY-001`     |
| Domain          | FEDRAMP                                  |
| Category        | SR                                       |
| Status          | Active                                   |
| Introduced In   | 1.0.0                                    |

## Description

Supply chain risk management policy and procedures are not developed, documented, and maintained to address purpose, scope, roles, responsibilities, management commitment, coordination, and compliance.

## Applicability

This signal applies to:

- Cloud Service Providers seeking FedRAMP authorization
- Federal agencies using cloud services
- Third-party assessment organizations (3PAOs)
- Federal information systems processing sensitive data

## FedRAMP Context (Informative)

**Control Family:** Supply Chain Risk Management (SR)
**Control:** SR-1
**Baseline:** Low, Moderate, High

**NIST 800-53 Text:** "Develop, document, and disseminate to [Assignment: organization-defined personnel or roles]: a. [Selection (one or more): Organization-level; Mission/business process-level; System-level] supply chain risk management policy that: 1. Addresses purpose, scope, roles, responsibilities, management commitment, coordination among organizational entities, and compliance; and 2. Is consistent with applicable laws, executive orders, directives, regulations, policies, standards, and guidelines; and b. Procedures to facilitate the implementation of the supply chain risk management policy and the associated supply chain risk management controls."

This reference is informative and does not constitute compliance guidance.

## Notes

Detection guidance: Look for missing supply chain risk management policy documentation, outdated or non-existent procedures, lack of policy dissemination evidence, absence of defined roles and responsibilities for supply chain risk management, and lack of integration with enterprise risk management programs. Check for missing references to applicable laws, regulations, and standards governing supply chain security.
