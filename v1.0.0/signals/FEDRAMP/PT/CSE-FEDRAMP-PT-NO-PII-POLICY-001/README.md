# CSE-FEDRAMP-PT-NO-PII-POLICY-001

**No PII Processing Policy**

## Signal Overview

| Field           | Value                                    |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------|
| Identifier      | `CSE-FEDRAMP-PT-NO-PII-POLICY-001`      |
| Domain          | FEDRAMP                                  |
| Category        | PT                                       |
| Status          | Active                                   |
| Introduced In   | 1.0.0                                    |

## Description

PII processing policy and procedures are not developed, documented, disseminated, and maintained to address purpose, scope, roles, responsibilities, management commitment, coordination, and compliance.

## Applicability

This signal applies to:

- Cloud Service Providers seeking FedRAMP authorization
- Federal agencies using cloud services
- Third-party assessment organizations (3PAOs)
- Systems processing federal information containing PII

## FedRAMP Context (Informative)

**Control Family:** PII Processing and Transparency (PT)
**Control:** PT-1
**Baseline:** Low, Moderate, High

**NIST 800-53 Text:** "Develop, document, and disseminate to [Assignment: organization-defined personnel or roles]: a. [Selection (one or more): Organization-level; Mission/business process-level; System-level] privacy policy that: 1. Addresses purpose, scope, roles, responsibilities, management commitment, coordination among organizational entities, and compliance; and 2. Is consistent with applicable laws, executive orders, directives, regulations, policies, standards, and guidelines; and b. Procedures to facilitate the implementation of the privacy policy and the associated privacy controls."

This reference is informative and does not constitute compliance guidance.

## Notes

Detection guidance: Look for missing PII processing policy documentation, outdated procedures, lack of policy dissemination evidence, absence of defined roles and responsibilities for PII processing, no documented coordination mechanisms, and missing compliance mappings to applicable privacy laws and regulations.
