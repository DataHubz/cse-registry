# CSE-FEDRAMP-RA-NO-TECHNICAL-SURVEILLANCE-005

**No Risk Response**

## Signal Overview

| Field           | Value                                         |
|-----------------|-----------------------------------------------|
| Identifier      | `CSE-FEDRAMP-RA-NO-TECHNICAL-SURVEILLANCE-005`|
| Domain          | FEDRAMP                                       |
| Category        | RA                                            |
| Status          | Active                                        |
| Introduced In   | 1.0.0                                         |

## Description

Risk response measures are not implemented to address identified risks, including acceptance, avoidance, mitigation, sharing, or transfer of risk.

## Applicability

This signal applies to:

- Cloud Service Providers seeking FedRAMP authorization
- Federal agencies using cloud services
- Third-party assessment organizations (3PAOs)
- Systems processing federal information

## FedRAMP Context (Informative)

**Control Family:** Risk Assessment (RA)
**Control:** RA-7
**Baseline:** Moderate, High

**NIST 800-53 Text:** "a. Develop a risk response plan that documents risk decisions and risk mitigation measures; and b. Review and update the risk response plan [Assignment: organization-defined frequency] or when there are significant changes to the organizational risk environment, the system, or other conditions that may impact the security or privacy state of the system."

This reference is informative and does not constitute compliance guidance.

## Notes

Detection guidance: Look for missing risk response plans, absence of documented risk decisions and mitigation measures, no evidence of risk acceptance, avoidance, mitigation, sharing, or transfer strategies, lack of regular risk response plan reviews and updates, and no process for responding to changes in the risk environment.
