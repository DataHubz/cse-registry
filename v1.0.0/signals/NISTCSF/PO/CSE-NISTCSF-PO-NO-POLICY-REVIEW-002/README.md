# CSE-NISTCSF-PO-NO-POLICY-REVIEW-002

**Cybersecurity Policy Not Reviewed and Updated**

## Signal Overview

| Field           | Value                                    |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------|
| Identifier      | `CSE-NISTCSF-PO-NO-POLICY-REVIEW-002` |
| Domain          | NISTCSF                                  |
| Category        | PO                                       |
| Status          | Active                                   |
| Introduced In   | 1.0.0                                    |

## Description

Cybersecurity policy is not reviewed, updated, and communicated.

## Applicability

This signal applies to:

- All organizations implementing cybersecurity risk management
- Enterprise governance and leadership functions
- Strategic planning and risk management teams
- Organizations subject to regulatory compliance requirements

## NIST CSF 2.0 Context (Informative)

**Function:** GOVERN (GV)
**Category:** GV.PO - Policy
**Subcategory:** GV.PO-02

**Framework Text:** "Policy for managing cybersecurity risks is reviewed, updated, communicated, and enforced to reflect changes in requirements, threats, technology, and organizational mission"

These references are informative and do not constitute compliance guidance.

## Notes

Detection guidance: Identify organizations with outdated cybersecurity policies, no documented policy review schedule, policies not updated to reflect current threat landscape or regulatory changes, lack of version control for policy documents, or absence of stakeholder communication when policies are updated.
