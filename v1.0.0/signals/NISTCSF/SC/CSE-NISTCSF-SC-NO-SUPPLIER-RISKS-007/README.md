# CSE-NISTCSF-SC-NO-SUPPLIER-RISKS-007

**Supplier Risks Not Understood and Managed**

## Signal Overview

| Field           | Value                                    |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------|
| Identifier      | `CSE-NISTCSF-SC-NO-SUPPLIER-RISKS-007` |
| Domain          | NISTCSF                                  |
| Category        | SC                                       |
| Status          | Active                                   |
| Introduced In   | 1.0.0                                    |

## Description

Risks posed by a supplier or other third party are not understood and managed.

## Applicability

This signal applies to:

- All organizations implementing cybersecurity risk management
- Organizations with supplier and third-party relationships
- Procurement and vendor management teams
- Supply chain security programs

## NIST CSF 2.0 Context (Informative)

**Function:** GOVERN (GV)
**Category:** GV.SC - Cybersecurity Supply Chain Risk Management
**Subcategory:** GV.SC-07

**Framework Text:** "The risks posed by a supplier, their products and services, and other third parties are understood, recorded, prioritized, assessed, responded to, and monitored over the course of the relationship"

These references are informative and do not constitute compliance guidance.

## Notes

Detection guidance: Look for the absence of ongoing risk management activities for suppliers throughout the relationship lifecycle. Check for lack of documentation, prioritization, assessment, response, or monitoring of supplier-related risks.
