# CSE-NISTCSF-SC-NO-SUPPLIER-REQUIREMENTS-005

**No Cybersecurity Requirements in Supply Chain Contracts**

## Signal Overview

| Field           | Value                                    |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------|
| Identifier      | `CSE-NISTCSF-SC-NO-SUPPLIER-REQUIREMENTS-005` |
| Domain          | NISTCSF                                  |
| Category        | SC                                       |
| Status          | Active                                   |
| Introduced In   | 1.0.0                                    |

## Description

Requirements to address cybersecurity risks in supply chains are not established and communicated.

## Applicability

This signal applies to:

- All organizations implementing cybersecurity risk management
- Organizations with supplier and third-party relationships
- Procurement and vendor management teams
- Supply chain security programs

## NIST CSF 2.0 Context (Informative)

**Function:** GOVERN (GV)
**Category:** GV.SC - Cybersecurity Supply Chain Risk Management
**Subcategory:** GV.SC-05

**Framework Text:** "Requirements to address cybersecurity risks in supply chains are established, prioritized, and integrated into contracts and other agreements with suppliers and other relevant third parties"

These references are informative and do not constitute compliance guidance.

## Notes

Detection guidance: Look for the absence of cybersecurity requirements in supplier contracts and agreements. Check for lack of prioritization of security requirements or missing integration of cybersecurity clauses in third-party agreements.
