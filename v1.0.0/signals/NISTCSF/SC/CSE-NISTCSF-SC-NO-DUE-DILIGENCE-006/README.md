# CSE-NISTCSF-SC-NO-DUE-DILIGENCE-006

**No Due Diligence on Prospective Suppliers**

## Signal Overview

| Field           | Value                                    |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------|
| Identifier      | `CSE-NISTCSF-SC-NO-DUE-DILIGENCE-006` |
| Domain          | NISTCSF                                  |
| Category        | SC                                       |
| Status          | Active                                   |
| Introduced In   | 1.0.0                                    |

## Description

Due diligence is not performed on prospective suppliers.

## Applicability

This signal applies to:

- All organizations implementing cybersecurity risk management
- Organizations with supplier and third-party relationships
- Procurement and vendor management teams
- Supply chain security programs

## NIST CSF 2.0 Context (Informative)

**Function:** GOVERN (GV)
**Category:** GV.SC - Cybersecurity Supply Chain Risk Management
**Subcategory:** GV.SC-06

**Framework Text:** "Planning and due diligence are performed to reduce risks before entering into formal supplier or other third-party relationships"

These references are informative and do not constitute compliance guidance.

## Notes

Detection guidance: Look for the absence of pre-contract due diligence processes for suppliers and third parties. Check for lack of planning activities or risk assessments conducted before establishing formal supplier relationships.
