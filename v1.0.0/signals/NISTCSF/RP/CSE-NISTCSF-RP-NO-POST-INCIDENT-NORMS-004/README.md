# CSE-NISTCSF-RP-NO-POST-INCIDENT-NORMS-004

**Critical Functions and Risk Not Considered for Post-Incident Norms**

## Signal Overview

| Field           | Value                                    |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------|
| Identifier      | `CSE-NISTCSF-RP-NO-POST-INCIDENT-NORMS-004` |
| Domain          | NISTCSF                                  |
| Category        | RP                                       |
| Status          | Active                                   |
| Introduced In   | 1.0.0                                    |

## Description

Critical mission functions and risk management are not considered to establish post-incident operational norms.

## Applicability

This signal applies to:

- All organizations with business continuity and disaster recovery requirements
- Incident recovery and restoration teams
- Communications and public relations functions
- Organizations with regulatory recovery requirements

## NIST CSF 2.0 Context (Informative)

**Function:** RECOVER (RC)
**Category:** RC.RP - Incident Recovery Plan Execution
**Subcategory:** RC.RP-04

**Framework Text:** "Critical mission functions and cybersecurity risk management are considered to establish post-incident operational norms"

These references are informative and do not constitute compliance guidance.

## Notes

Detection guidance: Identify recovery scenarios where operations resume without risk assessment updates or critical function reviews. Monitor for missing post-incident evaluations of acceptable risk levels, residual vulnerabilities, or changes to operational baselines following significant incidents.
