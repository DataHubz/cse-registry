# CSE-HIPAA-PRIVACY-NO-PRIVACY-CONTACT-019

**No Privacy Contact Person**

## Signal Overview

| Field           | Value                                    |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------|
| Identifier      | `CSE-HIPAA-PRIVACY-NO-PRIVACY-CONTACT-019` |
| Domain          | HIPAA                                    |
| Category        | PRIVACY                                   |
| Status          | Active                                   |
| Introduced In   | 1.0.0                                    |

## Description

No designated contact person/office for receiving complaints and providing information.

## Applicability

This signal applies to:

- Healthcare providers with direct patient relationships
- Health plans and insurers
- Healthcare clearinghouses
- Business associates handling PHI
- Patient-facing systems and portals
- Human resources and workforce management functions

## HIPAA Context (Informative)

**CFR Reference:** 45 CFR 164.530(a)(1)(ii)

**Regulatory Text:** "The covered entity must designate a contact person or office who is responsible for receiving complaints under this section and who is able to provide further information about matters covered by the notice required by § 164.520."

These references are informative and do not constitute compliance guidance.

## Notes

Detect absence of designated contact information in privacy notices, websites, or patient-facing materials. Verify that contact person/office information is current and accessible to individuals seeking to file complaints or obtain privacy information.
