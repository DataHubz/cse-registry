# CSE-HIPAA-PRIVACY-NO-USE-LIMITS-001

**No Limits on Uses and Disclosures**

## Signal Overview

| Field           | Value                                    |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------|
| Identifier      | `CSE-HIPAA-PRIVACY-NO-USE-LIMITS-001` |
| Domain          | HIPAA                                    |
| Category        | PRIVACY                                   |
| Status          | Active                                   |
| Introduced In   | 1.0.0                                    |

## Description

No limits on uses and disclosures of PHI; disclosures made beyond permitted or required purposes.

## Applicability

This signal applies to:

- Healthcare providers with direct patient relationships
- Health plans and insurers
- Healthcare clearinghouses
- Business associates handling PHI
- Patient-facing systems and portals
- Marketing and communications functions

## HIPAA Context (Informative)

**CFR Reference:** 45 CFR 164.502(a)

**Regulatory Text:** "A covered entity or business associate may not use or disclose protected health information, except as permitted or required by this subpart or by subpart C of part 160 of this subchapter."

These references are informative and do not constitute compliance guidance.

## Notes

Detection of this signal may occur when:
- PHI is used or disclosed for purposes not explicitly permitted by HIPAA
- Disclosures are made without proper authorization or legal basis
- PHI is shared beyond the scope of permitted purposes
- Monitoring reveals uses and disclosures that exceed regulatory permissions
