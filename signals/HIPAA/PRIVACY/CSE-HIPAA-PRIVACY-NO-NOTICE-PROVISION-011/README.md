# CSE-HIPAA-PRIVACY-NO-NOTICE-PROVISION-011

**No Notice Provision to Individuals**

## Signal Overview

| Field           | Value                                    |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------|
| Identifier      | `CSE-HIPAA-PRIVACY-NO-NOTICE-PROVISION-011` |
| Domain          | HIPAA                                    |
| Category        | PRIVACY                                   |
| Status          | Active                                   |
| Introduced In   | 1.0.0                                    |

## Description

Notice not provided at first service delivery, upon request, or as otherwise required.

## Applicability

This signal applies to:

- Healthcare providers with direct patient relationships
- Health plans and insurers
- Healthcare clearinghouses
- Business associates handling PHI
- Patient-facing systems and portals
- Marketing and communications functions

## HIPAA Context (Informative)

**CFR Reference:** 45 CFR 164.520(c)

**Regulatory Text:** "A covered entity must provide the notice required by this section: (i) No later than the date of the first service delivery; (ii) In an emergency treatment situation, as soon as reasonably practicable; (iii) Except for certain variations by type of covered entity, the covered entity must make a good faith effort to obtain a written acknowledgment of receipt."

These references are informative and do not constitute compliance guidance.

## Notes

Detection of this signal may occur when:
- Notice is not provided at first service delivery to new patients or plan enrollees
- Notice is not provided promptly after emergency treatment
- Notice is not made available electronically when individuals access PHI through electronic means
- Good faith effort to obtain written acknowledgment is not documented
- Notice is not provided within required timeframes when material changes occur
- Notice is not posted in clear and prominent locations in facilities
- Notice is not available upon request
- Health plans fail to provide notice at enrollment or within 60 days of material revision
