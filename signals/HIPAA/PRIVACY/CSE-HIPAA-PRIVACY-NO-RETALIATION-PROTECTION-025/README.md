# CSE-HIPAA-PRIVACY-NO-RETALIATION-PROTECTION-025

**No Retaliation Protection**

## Description

Intimidation or retaliation against individuals exercising their privacy rights.

## Applicability

This signal applies to:

- Healthcare providers with direct patient relationships
- Health plans and insurers
- Healthcare clearinghouses
- Business associates handling PHI
- Patient-facing systems and portals
- Human resources and workforce management functions

## HIPAA Context (Informative)

**CFR Reference:** 45 CFR 164.530(g)

**Regulatory Text:** "A covered entity may not intimidate, threaten, coerce, discriminate against, or take other retaliatory action against any individual for the exercise by the individual of any right established, or for participation in any process provided for, in this subpart or subpart D of this part, including the filing of a complaint under this section; or (ii) For testifying, assisting, or participating in an investigation, compliance review, proceeding, or hearing under Part C of title XI of the Act."

These references are informative and do not constitute compliance guidance.

## Notes

Detect adverse actions taken against individuals who exercised privacy rights, filed complaints, or participated in investigations. Monitor for patterns of denials of service, increased costs, or other retaliatory measures following privacy-related activities. Review complaints alleging intimidation or retaliation.
