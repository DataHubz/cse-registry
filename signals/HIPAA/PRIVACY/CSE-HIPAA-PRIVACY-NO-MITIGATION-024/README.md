# CSE-HIPAA-PRIVACY-NO-MITIGATION-024

**No Mitigation of Harmful Effects**

## Signal Overview

| Field           | Value                                    |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------|
| Identifier      | `CSE-HIPAA-PRIVACY-NO-MITIGATION-024` |
| Domain          | HIPAA                                    |
| Category        | PRIVACY                                   |
| Status          | Active                                   |
| Introduced In   | 1.0.0                                    |

## Description

No mitigation of known harmful effects of privacy violations.

## Applicability

This signal applies to:

- Healthcare providers with direct patient relationships
- Health plans and insurers
- Healthcare clearinghouses
- Business associates handling PHI
- Patient-facing systems and portals
- Human resources and workforce management functions

## HIPAA Context (Informative)

**CFR Reference:** 45 CFR 164.530(f)

**Regulatory Text:** "A covered entity must mitigate, to the extent practicable, any harmful effect that is known to the covered entity of a use or disclosure of protected health information in violation of its policies and procedures or the requirements of this subpart by the covered entity or its business associate."

These references are informative and do not constitute compliance guidance.

## Notes

Detect known privacy violations without documented mitigation efforts, failure to address harmful effects of unauthorized disclosures, or absence of remediation actions following privacy incidents. Monitor incident response procedures for mitigation steps taken to reduce harm to affected individuals.
