# CSE-HIPAA-PRIVACY-NO-ACCESS-RIGHTS-014

**No Individual Access Rights**

## Signal Overview

| Field           | Value                                    |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------|
| Identifier      | `CSE-HIPAA-PRIVACY-NO-ACCESS-RIGHTS-014` |
| Domain          | HIPAA                                    |
| Category        | PRIVACY                                   |
| Status          | Active                                   |
| Introduced In   | 1.0.0                                    |

## Description

Individuals not permitted to access and obtain copies of their PHI.

## Applicability

This signal applies to:

- Healthcare providers with direct patient relationships
- Health plans and insurers
- Healthcare clearinghouses
- Business associates handling PHI
- Patient-facing systems and portals
- Human resources and workforce management functions

## HIPAA Context (Informative)

**CFR Reference:** 45 CFR 164.524

**Regulatory Text:** "An individual has a right of access to inspect and obtain a copy of protected health information about the individual in a designated record set, for as long as the protected health information is maintained in the designated record set."

These references are informative and do not constitute compliance guidance.

## Notes

Detect absence of access request mechanisms, denied access requests without valid grounds for denial, or systems that do not provide individuals the ability to inspect and obtain copies of their PHI in designated record sets.
