# CSE-HIPAA-BREACH-NO-BURDEN-PROOF-008

**No Documentation for Burden of Proof**

## Signal Overview

| Field           | Value                                    |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------|
| Identifier      | `CSE-HIPAA-BREACH-NO-BURDEN-PROOF-008` |
| Domain          | HIPAA                                    |
| Category        | BREACH                                   |
| Status          | Active                                   |
| Introduced In   | 1.0.0                                    |

## Description

No documentation demonstrating notifications were made or breach assessment was performed.

## Applicability

This signal applies to:

- Covered entities experiencing data breaches
- Business associates handling ePHI
- Incident response and security teams
- Legal and compliance departments
- Public relations and communications functions
- Healthcare organizations with breach notification obligations

## HIPAA Context (Informative)

**CFR Reference:** 45 CFR 164.414(b)

**Regulatory Text:** "The covered entity or business associate, as applicable, shall have the burden of demonstrating that all notifications were made as required by this subpart or that the use or disclosure did not constitute a breach, as defined at § 164.402."

These references are informative and do not constitute compliance guidance.

## Notes

Detection guidance:

- Verify that comprehensive documentation exists for all breach incidents and responses
- Review records of notification delivery including dates, methods, and recipient confirmation
- Check for documented breach risk assessments that evaluate whether incidents constitute breaches
- Ensure documentation includes the basis for determining whether notification was required
- Audit evidence of notification methods used (written, electronic, substitute methods)
- Verify that documentation is sufficient to withstand regulatory scrutiny and demonstrate compliance
- Review retention policies to ensure breach documentation is maintained for required periods
- Check for documented risk assessment factors including likelihood of PHI compromise, type of information, unauthorized recipient, and mitigation efforts
