# CSE-HIPAA-ADMIN-NO-TERMINATION-PROC-008

**No Termination Procedures**

## Signal Overview

| Field           | Value                                    |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------|
| Identifier      | `CSE-HIPAA-ADMIN-NO-TERMINATION-PROC-008` |
| Domain          | HIPAA                                    |
| Category        | ADMIN                                   |
| Status          | Active                                   |
| Introduced In   | 1.0.0                                    |

## Description

No procedures exist for terminating access to electronic protected health information (ePHI) when the employment of, or other arrangement with, a workforce member ends or as required by determinations made as specified in workforce clearance procedures.

This signal indicates the absence of documented processes to revoke ePHI access promptly when workforce members leave or change roles.

## Applicability

This signal applies to:

- Covered entities (healthcare providers, health plans, healthcare clearinghouses)
- Business associates handling ePHI
- Health information systems and EHR platforms
- Cloud service providers hosting ePHI
- IT infrastructure supporting healthcare operations

## HIPAA Context (Informative)

**CFR Reference:** 45 CFR 164.308(a)(3)(ii)(C)
**Requirement Type:** Addressable

**Regulatory Text:** "Implement procedures for terminating access to electronic protected health information when the employment of, or other arrangement with, a workforce member ends or as required by determinations made as specified in paragraph (a)(3)(ii)(B) of this section."

These references are informative and do not constitute compliance guidance.

## Notes

Detection of this signal typically involves:

- Review of workforce termination procedures and checklists
- Examination of access revocation workflows and timelines
- Analysis of integration between HR systems and access management
- Verification of termination procedure execution records
- Assessment of access removal timeframes and completeness

The presence of this signal indicates that former workforce members may retain inappropriate access to ePHI.
