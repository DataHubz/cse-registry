# CSE-HIPAA-ADMIN-NO-RISK-MANAGEMENT-002

**No Risk Management**

## Signal Overview

| Field           | Value                                    |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------|
| Identifier      | `CSE-HIPAA-ADMIN-NO-RISK-MANAGEMENT-002` |
| Domain          | HIPAA                                    |
| Category        | ADMIN                                   |
| Status          | Active                                   |
| Introduced In   | 1.0.0                                    |

## Description

No security measures have been implemented to reduce risks and vulnerabilities to the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of electronic protected health information (ePHI) to a reasonable and appropriate level.

This signal indicates the absence of a risk management process that implements safeguards to mitigate identified risks from the risk analysis.

## Applicability

This signal applies to:

- Covered entities (healthcare providers, health plans, healthcare clearinghouses)
- Business associates handling ePHI
- Health information systems and EHR platforms
- Cloud service providers hosting ePHI
- IT infrastructure supporting healthcare operations

## HIPAA Context (Informative)

**CFR Reference:** 45 CFR 164.308(a)(1)(ii)(B)
**Requirement Type:** Required

**Regulatory Text:** "Implement security measures sufficient to reduce risks and vulnerabilities to a reasonable and appropriate level to comply with 164.306(a)."

These references are informative and do not constitute compliance guidance.

## Notes

Detection of this signal typically involves:

- Review of risk management plans and remediation activities
- Examination of implemented security controls and safeguards
- Analysis of risk treatment decisions and mitigation strategies
- Verification of security measure effectiveness
- Assessment of residual risk levels

The presence of this signal indicates that identified risks are not being actively managed or mitigated.
