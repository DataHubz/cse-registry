# CSE-HIPAA-ADMIN-NO-EMERGENCY-MODE-018

**No Emergency Mode Operation Plan**

## Signal Overview

| Field           | Value                                    |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------|
| Identifier      | `CSE-HIPAA-ADMIN-NO-EMERGENCY-MODE-018` |
| Domain          | HIPAA                                    |
| Category        | ADMIN                                   |
| Status          | Active                                   |
| Introduced In   | 1.0.0                                    |

## Description

No procedures exist to enable continuation of critical business processes for protection of the security of electronic protected health information (ePHI) while operating in emergency mode during system failures or natural disasters.

This signal indicates the absence of documented emergency operations procedures to maintain ePHI security during contingency situations.

## Applicability

This signal applies to:

- Covered entities (healthcare providers, health plans, healthcare clearinghouses)
- Business associates handling ePHI
- Health information systems and EHR platforms
- Cloud service providers hosting ePHI
- IT infrastructure supporting healthcare operations

## HIPAA Context (Informative)

**CFR Reference:** 45 CFR 164.308(a)(7)(ii)(C)
**Requirement Type:** Required

**Regulatory Text:** "Establish (and implement as needed) procedures to enable continuation of critical business processes for protection of the security of electronic protected health information while operating in emergency mode."

These references are informative and do not constitute compliance guidance.

## Notes

Detection of this signal typically involves:

- Review of emergency mode operation plans and procedures
- Examination of critical business process identification and prioritization
- Analysis of emergency mode security controls and safeguards
- Verification of emergency operations testing and exercises
- Assessment of transition procedures between normal and emergency modes

The presence of this signal indicates that ePHI security may be compromised during emergency operations or system outages.
