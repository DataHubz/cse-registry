# CSE-HIPAA-ORG-NO-BA-CONTRACT-001

**No Business Associate Contracts**

## Signal Overview

| Field           | Value                                    |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------|
| Identifier      | `CSE-HIPAA-ORG-NO-BA-CONTRACT-001` |
| Domain          | HIPAA                                    |
| Category        | ORG                                   |
| Status          | Active                                   |
| Introduced In   | 1.0.0                                    |

## Description

No written contract or arrangement with business associates requiring compliance with applicable Security Rule requirements.

## Applicability

This signal applies to:

- Covered entities and business associates
- Healthcare organizations with business associate relationships
- Group health plans and plan sponsors
- Organizations requiring HIPAA compliance documentation
- Vendor management and contract administration functions

## HIPAA Context (Informative)

**CFR Reference:** 45 CFR 164.314(a)(2)(i)
**Requirement Type:** Required

**Regulatory Text:** "A covered entity, in accordance with section 164.308(b)(4), may permit a business associate to create, receive, maintain, or transmit electronic protected health information on the covered entity's behalf only if the covered entity obtains satisfactory assurances, in accordance with section 164.314(a)(1), that the business associate will appropriately safeguard the information. A covered entity must document the satisfactory assurances required by paragraph (a)(1) of this section through a written contract or other arrangement with the business associate that meets the applicable requirements of section 164.314(a)(2)."

These references are informative and do not constitute compliance guidance.

## Notes

Detection guidance: Review vendor and third-party relationships to identify business associates who create, receive, maintain, or transmit ePHI. Verify that written Business Associate Agreements (BAAs) exist for all such relationships and include required Security Rule safeguards. Check contract management systems and procurement processes for gaps in BAA coverage.
