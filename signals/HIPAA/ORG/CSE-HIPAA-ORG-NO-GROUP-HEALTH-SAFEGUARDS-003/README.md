# CSE-HIPAA-ORG-NO-GROUP-HEALTH-SAFEGUARDS-003

**No Group Health Plan Safeguards**

## Signal Overview

| Field           | Value                                    |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------|
| Identifier      | `CSE-HIPAA-ORG-NO-GROUP-HEALTH-SAFEGUARDS-003` |
| Domain          | HIPAA                                    |
| Category        | ORG                                   |
| Status          | Active                                   |
| Introduced In   | 1.0.0                                    |

## Description

Group health plans do not ensure plan sponsor implements required safeguards.

## Applicability

This signal applies to:

- Covered entities and business associates
- Healthcare organizations with business associate relationships
- Group health plans and plan sponsors
- Organizations requiring HIPAA compliance documentation
- Vendor management and contract administration functions

## HIPAA Context (Informative)

**CFR Reference:** 45 CFR 164.314(b)(2)
**Requirement Type:** Required

**Regulatory Text:** "A group health plan that provides for access to electronic protected health information by a plan sponsor must ensure that the plan documents restrict the uses and disclosures of such information by the plan sponsor consistent with the requirements of this subpart; establish the permitted and required uses and disclosures of such information by the plan sponsor; provide that the plan sponsor will not use or further disclose the information other than as permitted or required by the plan documents or as required by law; require the plan sponsor to ensure that any agents to whom it provides electronic protected health information agree to implement reasonable and appropriate security measures to protect the information; establish the permitted and required uses and disclosures by such agents; and require the plan sponsor to report to the group health plan any security incident of which it becomes aware."

These references are informative and do not constitute compliance guidance.

## Notes

Detection guidance: For group health plans, review plan documents to confirm they include provisions restricting plan sponsor access to ePHI and requiring appropriate safeguards. Verify that plan sponsors have implemented security measures and incident reporting procedures. Assess whether plan amendments have been executed to incorporate required Security Rule provisions.
