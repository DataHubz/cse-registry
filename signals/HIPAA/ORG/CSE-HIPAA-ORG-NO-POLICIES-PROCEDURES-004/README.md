# CSE-HIPAA-ORG-NO-POLICIES-PROCEDURES-004

**No Policies and Procedures**

## Signal Overview

| Field           | Value                                    |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------|
| Identifier      | `CSE-HIPAA-ORG-NO-POLICIES-PROCEDURES-004` |
| Domain          | HIPAA                                    |
| Category        | ORG                                   |
| Status          | Active                                   |
| Introduced In   | 1.0.0                                    |

## Description

No reasonable and appropriate policies and procedures to comply with Security Rule standards.

## Applicability

This signal applies to:

- Covered entities and business associates
- Healthcare organizations with business associate relationships
- Group health plans and plan sponsors
- Organizations requiring HIPAA compliance documentation
- Vendor management and contract administration functions

## HIPAA Context (Informative)

**CFR Reference:** 45 CFR 164.316(a)
**Requirement Type:** Required

**Regulatory Text:** "A covered entity or business associate must, in accordance with section 164.306: (a) Implement reasonable and appropriate policies and procedures to comply with the standards, implementation specifications, or other requirements of this subpart, taking into account those factors specified in section 164.306(b)(2). This standard is not to be construed to permit or excuse an action that violates any other standard, implementation specification, or other requirement of this subpart. A covered entity or business associate may change its policies and procedures at any time, provided that the changes are documented and are implemented in accordance with this subpart."

These references are informative and do not constitute compliance guidance.

## Notes

Detection guidance: Assess whether the organization has documented policies and procedures addressing all required and applicable Security Rule standards and implementation specifications. Review policy inventory and governance processes to identify gaps in coverage. Verify that policies are reasonable and appropriate given the organization's size, complexity, and technical infrastructure as required by the scalability principle.
