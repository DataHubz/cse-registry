# CSE-FEDRAMP-SA-NO-ACQUISITION-POLICY-001

**No System Acquisition Policy**

## Signal Overview

| Field           | Value                                    |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------|
| Identifier      | `CSE-FEDRAMP-SA-NO-ACQUISITION-POLICY-001` |
| Domain          | FEDRAMP                                  |
| Category        | SA                                       |
| Status          | Active                                   |
| Introduced In   | 1.0.0                                    |

## Description

System and services acquisition policy and procedures are not developed, documented, and maintained.

## Applicability

This signal applies to:

- Cloud Service Providers seeking FedRAMP authorization
- Federal agencies using cloud services
- Third-party assessment organizations (3PAOs)
- Systems processing federal information

## FedRAMP Context (Informative)

**Control Family:** System and Services Acquisition (SA)
**Control:** SA-1
**Baseline:** Low, Moderate, High

**NIST 800-53 Text:** "Develop, document, and disseminate to [Assignment: organization-defined personnel or roles] a system and services acquisition policy that addresses purpose, scope, roles, responsibilities, management commitment, coordination among organizational entities, and compliance; and procedures to facilitate the implementation of the system and services acquisition policy and associated system and services acquisition controls."

This reference is informative and does not constitute compliance guidance.

## Notes

Detection guidance: Look for missing system and services acquisition policy documentation, outdated procedures, lack of policy dissemination evidence, absence of defined roles and responsibilities for acquisition activities, and missing documentation of acquisition processes and security requirements integration.
