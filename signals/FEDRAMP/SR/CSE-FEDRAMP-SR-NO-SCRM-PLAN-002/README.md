# CSE-FEDRAMP-SR-NO-SCRM-PLAN-002

**No Supply Chain Risk Management Plan**

## Signal Overview

| Field           | Value                                    |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------|
| Identifier      | `CSE-FEDRAMP-SR-NO-SCRM-PLAN-002`       |
| Domain          | FEDRAMP                                  |
| Category        | SR                                       |
| Status          | Active                                   |
| Introduced In   | 1.0.0                                    |

## Description

A comprehensive supply chain risk management plan addressing identification, assessment, and mitigation of supply chain risks is not developed, documented, implemented, and maintained.

## Applicability

This signal applies to:

- Cloud Service Providers seeking FedRAMP authorization
- Federal agencies using cloud services
- Third-party assessment organizations (3PAOs)
- Federal information systems processing sensitive data

## FedRAMP Context (Informative)

**Control Family:** Supply Chain Risk Management (SR)
**Control:** SR-2
**Baseline:** Moderate, High

**NIST 800-53 Text:** "Develop a plan for managing supply chain risks associated with the research and development, design, manufacturing, acquisition, delivery, integration, operations and maintenance, and disposal of the following systems, system components or system services: [Assignment: organization-defined systems, system components, or system services]; b. Review and update the supply chain risk management plan [Assignment: organization-defined frequency] or as required, to address threat, organizational or environmental changes; and c. Protect the supply chain risk management plan from unauthorized disclosure and modification."

This reference is informative and does not constitute compliance guidance.

## Notes

Detection guidance: Look for absence of a documented supply chain risk management plan, lack of risk assessment processes for suppliers and vendors, missing threat intelligence integration, absence of supply chain risk monitoring procedures, and lack of plan review and update mechanisms. Verify that the plan addresses the full system lifecycle from acquisition through disposal and includes protection measures for the plan itself.
