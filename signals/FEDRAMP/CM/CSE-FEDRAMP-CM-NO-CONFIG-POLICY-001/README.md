# CSE-FEDRAMP-CM-NO-CONFIG-POLICY-001

**No Configuration Management Policy**

## Signal Overview

| Field           | Value                                        |
|-----------------|----------------------------------------------|
| Identifier      | `CSE-FEDRAMP-CM-NO-CONFIG-POLICY-001`       |
| Domain          | FEDRAMP                                      |
| Category        | CM                                           |
| Status          | Active                                       |
| Introduced In   | 1.0.0                                        |

## Description

No configuration management policy and procedures have been documented or implemented to govern the development, documentation, and dissemination of configuration management practices.

## Applicability

This signal applies to:

- Cloud Service Providers (CSPs) seeking FedRAMP authorization
- Federal agencies operating cloud systems
- Third Party Assessment Organizations (3PAOs) conducting assessments
- Federal information systems processing government data

## FedRAMP Context (Informative)

**Control Family:** Configuration Management (CM)
**Control Number:** CM-1
**FedRAMP Baseline:** Low, Moderate, High

**NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5 Text:** "The organization develops, documents, and disseminates to [Assignment: organization-defined personnel or roles]: a. A configuration management policy that addresses purpose, scope, roles, responsibilities, management commitment, coordination among organizational entities, and compliance; and b. Procedures to facilitate the implementation of the configuration management policy and associated configuration management controls."

These references are informative and do not constitute compliance guidance.

## Notes

Detection guidance: Review system documentation repositories, policy management systems, and governance frameworks to determine if formal configuration management policies and procedures exist. Check for documented approval workflows, version control of policies, and evidence of dissemination to relevant personnel. Look for absence of policy documents, outdated policies, or lack of defined procedures for configuration management activities.
