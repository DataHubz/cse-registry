# CSE-FEDRAMP-PE-NO-PHYSICAL-POLICY-001

**No Physical Security Policy**

## Signal Overview

| Field           | Value                                    |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------|
| Identifier      | `CSE-FEDRAMP-PE-NO-PHYSICAL-POLICY-001` |
| Domain          | FEDRAMP                                  |
| Category        | PE                                       |
| Status          | Active                                   |
| Introduced In   | 1.0.0                                    |

## Description

Physical and environmental protection policy and procedures are not developed, documented, and maintained.

## Applicability

This signal applies to:

- Cloud Service Providers seeking FedRAMP authorization
- Federal agencies using cloud services
- Third-party assessment organizations (3PAOs)
- Systems processing federal information

## FedRAMP Context (Informative)

**Control Family:** Physical and Environmental Protection (PE)
**Control:** PE-1
**Baseline:** Low, Moderate, High

**NIST 800-53 Text:** "Develop, document, and disseminate a physical and environmental protection policy... and procedures to facilitate the implementation of the physical and environmental protection policy and the associated physical and environmental protection controls."

This reference is informative and does not constitute compliance guidance.

## Notes

Detection guidance: Look for missing physical security policy documentation, outdated procedures, lack of policy dissemination evidence, absence of defined roles and responsibilities for physical security, and missing review/update schedules.
