**No Security Training Policy**

## Description

Security and privacy literacy training policy and procedures are not established.

## Applicability

This signal applies to:

- Cloud Service Providers seeking FedRAMP authorization
- Federal agencies using cloud services
- Third-party assessment organizations (3PAOs)
- Systems processing federal information

## FedRAMP Context (Informative)

**Control Family:** Awareness and Training (AT)
**Control:** AT-1
**Baseline:** Low, Moderate, High

**NIST 800-53 Text:** "Develop, document, and disseminate a security and privacy awareness and training policy... and procedures."

This reference is informative and does not constitute compliance guidance.

## Notes

Detection guidance: Look for missing training policy documentation, absence of training procedures, and lack of defined training requirements.
