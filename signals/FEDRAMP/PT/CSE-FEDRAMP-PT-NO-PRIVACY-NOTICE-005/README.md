# CSE-FEDRAMP-PT-NO-PRIVACY-NOTICE-005

**No Privacy Notice**

## Signal Overview

| Field           | Value                                    |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------|
| Identifier      | `CSE-FEDRAMP-PT-NO-PRIVACY-NOTICE-005`  |
| Domain          | FEDRAMP                                  |
| Category        | PT                                       |
| Status          | Active                                   |
| Introduced In   | 1.0.0                                    |

## Description

The organization does not provide privacy notices to individuals that describe what PII is collected, how it is used, maintained, and shared, and individual privacy rights and choices.

## Applicability

This signal applies to:

- Cloud Service Providers seeking FedRAMP authorization
- Federal agencies using cloud services
- Third-party assessment organizations (3PAOs)
- Systems processing federal information containing PII

## FedRAMP Context (Informative)

**Control Family:** PII Processing and Transparency (PT)
**Control:** PT-5
**Baseline:** Low, Moderate, High

**NIST 800-53 Text:** "Provide notice to individuals about the processing of personally identifiable information that: a. Is available to individuals upon first interacting with an organization, and subsequently at [Assignment: organization-defined frequency]; b. Is clear and easy-to-understand, expressing information about personally identifiable information processing in plain language; c. Identifies the authority that authorizes the processing of personally identifiable information; d. Identifies the purposes for which personally identifiable information is to be processed; and e. Includes [Assignment: organization-defined information]."

This reference is informative and does not constitute compliance guidance.

## Notes

Detection guidance: Look for missing privacy notices on systems and applications, outdated notices, unclear or overly complex language that is not plain language, missing required elements (authority, purposes, PII types, retention, sharing practices), lack of notice accessibility at first interaction, no evidence of periodic notice updates, and absence of individual rights information.
