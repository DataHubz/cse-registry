# CSE-FEDRAMP-PT-NO-PII-AUTHORITY-002

**No Authority to Process PII**

## Signal Overview

| Field           | Value                                    |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------|
| Identifier      | `CSE-FEDRAMP-PT-NO-PII-AUTHORITY-002`   |
| Domain          | FEDRAMP                                  |
| Category        | PT                                       |
| Status          | Active                                   |
| Introduced In   | 1.0.0                                    |

## Description

The organization does not determine and document the legal authority to collect, use, maintain, and share PII, or operates outside the documented authority.

## Applicability

This signal applies to:

- Cloud Service Providers seeking FedRAMP authorization
- Federal agencies using cloud services
- Third-party assessment organizations (3PAOs)
- Systems processing federal information containing PII

## FedRAMP Context (Informative)

**Control Family:** PII Processing and Transparency (PT)
**Control:** PT-2
**Baseline:** Low, Moderate, High

**NIST 800-53 Text:** "Determine and document the [Assignment: organization-defined authority] that permits the [Assignment: organization-defined processing] of personally identifiable information (PII)."

This reference is informative and does not constitute compliance guidance.

## Notes

Detection guidance: Look for missing legal authority documentation, absence of citations to statutory or regulatory authorities, lack of documented permissions for PII collection and processing, no evidence of authority review or validation, and processing activities that exceed documented authorities.
