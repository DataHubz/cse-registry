# CSE-FEDRAMP-PM-NO-RISK-MGMT-006

**No Risk Management Strategy**

## Signal Overview

| Field           | Value                                          |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------------|
| Identifier      | `CSE-FEDRAMP-PM-NO-RISK-MGMT-006`              |
| Domain          | FEDRAMP                                        |
| Category        | PM                                             |
| Status          | Active                                         |
| Introduced In   | 1.0.0                                          |

## Description

Organization-wide risk management strategy is not developed, documented, or implemented.

This signal indicates that the organization may lack a comprehensive, enterprise-wide strategy for managing information security, privacy, and supply chain risks, which is essential for consistent risk-based decision making across the organization.

## Applicability

This signal applies to:

- Cloud Service Providers (CSPs) pursuing FedRAMP authorization
- Federal agencies managing information systems
- Third Party Assessment Organizations (3PAOs) conducting assessments
- Federal information systems processing, storing, or transmitting federal data
- Organizations supporting federal cloud services

## FedRAMP Context (Informative)

### Control Family
Program Management (PM)

### Control
PM-9: Risk Management Strategy

### Baseline
Low, Moderate, High

### NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5
The organization:
- Develops a comprehensive strategy to manage:
  - Security risk to organizational operations and assets, individuals, other organizations, and the Nation associated with the operation and use of organizational systems
  - Privacy risk to individuals resulting from the authorized processing of personally identifiable information
  - Supply chain risk to organizational operations and assets, individuals, other organizations, and the Nation associated with the research and development, design, manufacturing, acquisition, delivery, integration, operations and maintenance, and disposal of systems and system components
- Implements the risk management strategy consistently across the organization
- Reviews and updates the risk management strategy [FedRAMP Assignment: at least annually] or as required, to address organizational changes

These references are informative and do not constitute compliance guidance.

## Notes

Detection of this signal typically involves:

- Review of organization-wide risk management strategy documentation
- Analysis of strategy scope covering security, privacy, and supply chain risks
- Verification of risk management framework and methodology definition
- Assessment of risk appetite, tolerance, and acceptance criteria
- Examination of roles and responsibilities for risk management activities
- Review of risk assessment and analysis processes
- Verification of risk response strategies and decision-making criteria
- Assessment of risk monitoring and reporting mechanisms
- Evaluation of strategy implementation consistency across the organization
- Review of strategy alignment with organizational mission and business objectives
- Verification of annual review and update processes
- Assessment of strategy integration with enterprise risk management
- Evaluation of stakeholder engagement in risk management processes
- Review of risk communication and escalation procedures

The presence of this signal indicates a condition that warrants review in the context of FedRAMP program management requirements.
