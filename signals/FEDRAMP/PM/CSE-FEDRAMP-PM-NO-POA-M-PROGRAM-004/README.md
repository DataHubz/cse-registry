# CSE-FEDRAMP-PM-NO-POA-M-PROGRAM-004

**No Plan of Action and Milestones Process**

## Signal Overview

| Field           | Value                                          |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------------|
| Identifier      | `CSE-FEDRAMP-PM-NO-POA-M-PROGRAM-004`          |
| Domain          | FEDRAMP                                        |
| Category        | PM                                             |
| Status          | Active                                         |
| Introduced In   | 1.0.0                                          |

## Description

Organization-wide plan of action and milestones (POA&M) process is not implemented for tracking and managing security weaknesses and deficiencies.

This signal indicates that the organization may lack a systematic, organization-wide process for identifying, tracking, and remediating security weaknesses, vulnerabilities, and control deficiencies across all organizational systems.

## Applicability

This signal applies to:

- Cloud Service Providers (CSPs) pursuing FedRAMP authorization
- Federal agencies managing information systems
- Third Party Assessment Organizations (3PAOs) conducting assessments
- Federal information systems processing, storing, or transmitting federal data
- Organizations supporting federal cloud services

## FedRAMP Context (Informative)

### Control Family
Program Management (PM)

### Control
PM-4: Plan of Action and Milestones Process

### Baseline
Low, Moderate, High

### NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5
The organization:
- Implements a process to ensure that plans of action and milestones for the information security, privacy, and supply chain risk management programs and associated organizational systems:
  - Are developed and maintained
  - Document the remedial information security, privacy, and supply chain risk management actions to adequately respond to risk to organizational operations and assets, individuals, other organizations, and the Nation
  - Are reported in accordance with established reporting requirements
- Reviews plans of action and milestones for consistency with the organizational risk management strategy and organization-wide priorities for risk response actions

These references are informative and do not constitute compliance guidance.

## Notes

Detection of this signal typically involves:

- Review of POA&M process documentation and procedures
- Analysis of POA&M tracking system or tool implementation
- Verification of POA&M development and maintenance practices
- Assessment of POA&M completeness and accuracy across all systems
- Examination of remedial action documentation and timelines
- Review of POA&M reporting mechanisms and frequency
- Verification of POA&M review and approval workflows
- Assessment of POA&M consistency with risk management strategy
- Evaluation of POA&M alignment with organizational risk response priorities
- Review of POA&M metrics and status tracking
- Verification of closure criteria and validation processes
- Assessment of integration with continuous monitoring and assessment processes

The presence of this signal indicates a condition that warrants review in the context of FedRAMP program management requirements.
