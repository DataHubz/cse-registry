# CSE-HITRUST-PHYSICAL-NO-EQUIPMENT-PROTECTION-004

**Equipment Protection Missing**

## Signal Overview

| Field           | Value                                    |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------|
| Identifier      | `CSE-HITRUST-PHYSICAL-NO-EQUIPMENT-PROTECTION-004` |
| Domain          | HITRUST                                  |
| Category        | PHYSICAL                                 |
| Status          | Active                                   |
| Introduced In   | 1.0.0                                    |

## Description

No physical protection measures in place for equipment against environmental threats and unauthorized access.

## Applicability

This signal applies to:

- Servers and network equipment
- Data storage systems
- Workstations and laptops
- Mobile devices with access to sensitive information
- Backup media and systems

## HITRUST Context (Informative)

**Control Domain:** 08 - Physical and Environmental Security

**Reference:** 08.d

**Regulatory Text:** "Equipment shall be physically protected from security threats and environmental hazards."

These references are informative and do not constitute compliance guidance.

## Notes

Detection guidance: Review physical protection measures for equipment including rack security, cable locks, secure mounting, and environmental controls. Verify protection against theft, tampering, and environmental hazards such as fire, water, temperature extremes, and power fluctuations. Check for equipment placement strategies that minimize exposure to physical threats and ensure appropriate environmental monitoring.
