# CSE-HITRUST-PHYSICAL-NO-MAINTENANCE-007

**Equipment Maintenance Missing**

## Signal Overview

| Field           | Value                                    |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------|
| Identifier      | `CSE-HITRUST-PHYSICAL-NO-MAINTENANCE-007` |
| Domain          | HITRUST                                  |
| Category        | PHYSICAL                                 |
| Status          | Active                                   |
| Introduced In   | 1.0.0                                    |

## Description

No equipment maintenance procedures in place to ensure the continuing availability and integrity of information processing facilities.

## Applicability

This signal applies to:

- Servers and network infrastructure
- Data storage systems
- Environmental control systems
- Physical security systems
- Supporting utilities and backup systems

## HITRUST Context (Informative)

**Control Domain:** 08 - Physical and Environmental Security

**Reference:** 08.g

**Regulatory Text:** "Equipment shall be correctly maintained to ensure its continuing availability and integrity."

These references are informative and do not constitute compliance guidance.

## Notes

Detection guidance: Review equipment maintenance procedures including preventive maintenance schedules, maintenance records, and vendor service agreements. Verify that maintenance activities are documented and performed by authorized personnel. Check for controls over maintenance performed by third parties, requirements for escorting maintenance personnel, and procedures to ensure that equipment is properly sanitized or secured during maintenance activities.
