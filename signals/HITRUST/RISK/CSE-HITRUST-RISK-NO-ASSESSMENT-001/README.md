# CSE-HITRUST-RISK-NO-ASSESSMENT-001

**Risk Assessment Not Conducted**

## Signal Overview

| Field           | Value                                    |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------|
| Identifier      | `CSE-HITRUST-RISK-NO-ASSESSMENT-001` |
| Domain          | HITRUST                                  |
| Category        | RISK                                     |
| Status          | Active                                   |
| Introduced In   | 1.0.0                                    |

## Description

Organization has not conducted a comprehensive risk assessment to identify and evaluate risks to the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of information assets and systems. Risk assessments must systematically identify threats, vulnerabilities, likelihood, and potential impacts to enable informed risk management decisions.

## Applicability

This signal applies to:

- Healthcare organizations subject to HIPAA requirements
- Organizations handling sensitive health information
- Entities subject to HITRUST CSF certification
- Cloud service providers in healthcare sector
- Business associates and covered entities
- Organizations with regulated information assets

## HITRUST CSF Context (Informative)

**Control Reference:** 03.a - Risk Assessment

**Control Domain:** 03 - Risk Management

**Framework Text:** Organizations must conduct risk assessments to identify risks to information assets, determine their likelihood and impact, and establish risk treatment priorities. Risk assessments should be performed regularly, following organizational changes, or when new threats emerge.

These references are informative and do not constitute compliance guidance.

## Related Signals

- `CSE-NISTCSF-RA-NO-IMPACT-LIKELIHOOD-004` (NIST CSF risk impact and likelihood)
- `CSE-ISO27001-ORG-NO-RISK-ASSESSMENT` (ISO 27001 risk assessment requirement)

## Notes

Detection of this signal typically involves:

- Review of documented risk assessment procedures and methodologies
- Examination of risk registers and assessment reports
- Verification of assessment scope covering all information assets
- Analysis of threat and vulnerability identification processes
- Assessment of risk evaluation criteria and rating scales
- Validation of stakeholder involvement in risk assessment activities

The presence of this signal indicates that the organization may be operating without understanding its risk exposure, potentially leading to inadequate security controls and compliance violations.
