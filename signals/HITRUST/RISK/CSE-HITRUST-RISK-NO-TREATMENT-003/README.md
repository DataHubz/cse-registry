# CSE-HITRUST-RISK-NO-TREATMENT-003

**Risk Treatment Plan Missing**

## Signal Overview

| Field           | Value                                    |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------|
| Identifier      | `CSE-HITRUST-RISK-NO-TREATMENT-003` |
| Domain          | HITRUST                                  |
| Category        | RISK                                     |
| Status          | Active                                   |
| Introduced In   | 1.0.0                                    |

## Description

Organization has not developed a risk treatment plan to address identified risks through mitigation, transfer, avoidance, or acceptance strategies. Risk treatment plans must document selected risk responses, assigned responsibilities, required resources, timelines, and expected outcomes for each significant risk.

## Applicability

This signal applies to:

- Healthcare organizations subject to HIPAA requirements
- Organizations handling sensitive health information
- Entities subject to HITRUST CSF certification
- Cloud service providers in healthcare sector
- Business associates and covered entities
- Organizations with identified security and privacy risks

## HITRUST CSF Context (Informative)

**Control Reference:** 03.b - Risk Treatment

**Control Domain:** 03 - Risk Management

**Framework Text:** Organizations must develop and implement risk treatment plans that define how identified risks will be addressed. Risk treatment options include implementing controls to reduce risk (mitigation), sharing risk with third parties (transfer), avoiding the risk-producing activity, or accepting the risk. Treatment plans should specify actions, responsibilities, timelines, and success criteria.

These references are informative and do not constitute compliance guidance.

## Notes

Detection of this signal typically involves:

- Review of documented risk treatment plans and action items
- Examination of risk response strategies (mitigate, transfer, avoid, accept)
- Verification of assigned responsibilities and ownership for risk treatment
- Analysis of timelines and milestones for risk mitigation activities
- Assessment of resource allocation for risk treatment implementation
- Validation of treatment effectiveness monitoring processes

The presence of this signal indicates that identified risks may remain unaddressed, potentially exposing the organization to security incidents, compliance violations, and business disruptions.
