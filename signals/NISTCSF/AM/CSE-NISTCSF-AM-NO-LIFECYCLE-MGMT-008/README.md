# CSE-NISTCSF-AM-NO-LIFECYCLE-MGMT-008

**Systems and Assets Not Managed Throughout Life Cycles**

## Signal Overview

| Field           | Value                                    |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------|
| Identifier      | `CSE-NISTCSF-AM-NO-LIFECYCLE-MGMT-008` |
| Domain          | NISTCSF                                  |

| Introduced In   | 1.0.0                                    |

## Description

Systems, hardware, software, services, and data are not managed throughout their life cycles.

## Applicability

This signal applies to:

- All organizations implementing cybersecurity risk management
- IT asset management and inventory teams
- Configuration management programs
- Organizations maintaining technology inventories

## NIST CSF 2.0 Context (Informative)

**Function:** IDENTIFY (ID)
**Category:** ID.AM - Asset Management
**Subcategory:** ID.AM-08

**Framework Text:** "Systems, hardware, software, services, and data are managed throughout their life cycles"

These references are informative and do not constitute compliance guidance.

## Notes

Detection guidance: Monitor for absence of lifecycle management processes, missing decommissioning procedures, lack of secure disposal practices, or gaps in cradle-to-grave asset tracking. Organizations should implement lifecycle management that includes acquisition, deployment, maintenance, retirement, and secure disposal phases with appropriate security controls at each stage.
