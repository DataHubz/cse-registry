# CSE-NISTCSF-AM-NO-SUPPLIER-INVENTORY-004

**No Supplier Services Inventory Maintained**

## Signal Overview

| Field           | Value                                    |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------|
| Identifier      | `CSE-NISTCSF-AM-NO-SUPPLIER-INVENTORY-004` |
| Domain          | NISTCSF                                  |
| Category        | AM                                       |
| Status          | Active                                   |
| Introduced In   | 1.0.0                                    |

## Description

Inventories of services provided by suppliers are not maintained.

## Applicability

This signal applies to:

- All organizations implementing cybersecurity risk management
- IT asset management and inventory teams
- Configuration management programs
- Organizations maintaining technology inventories

## NIST CSF 2.0 Context (Informative)

**Function:** IDENTIFY (ID)
**Category:** ID.AM - Asset Management
**Subcategory:** ID.AM-04

**Framework Text:** "Inventories of services provided by suppliers are maintained"

These references are informative and do not constitute compliance guidance.

## Notes

Detection guidance: Monitor for absence of vendor/supplier registries, missing third-party service catalogs, lack of supply chain documentation, or gaps in external service provider tracking. Organizations should maintain comprehensive inventories that include cloud service providers, managed service providers, contractors, outsourced services, and critical suppliers.
