# CSE-NISTCSF-AM-NO-HARDWARE-INVENTORY-001

**No Hardware Inventory Maintained**

## Signal Overview

| Field           | Value                                    |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------|
| Identifier      | `CSE-NISTCSF-AM-NO-HARDWARE-INVENTORY-001` |
| Domain          | NISTCSF                                  |
| Category        | AM                                       |
| Status          | Active                                   |
| Introduced In   | 1.0.0                                    |

## Description

Inventories of hardware managed by the organization are not maintained.

## Applicability

This signal applies to:

- All organizations implementing cybersecurity risk management
- IT asset management and inventory teams
- Configuration management programs
- Organizations maintaining technology inventories

## NIST CSF 2.0 Context (Informative)

**Function:** IDENTIFY (ID)
**Category:** ID.AM - Asset Management
**Subcategory:** ID.AM-01

**Framework Text:** "Inventories of hardware managed by the organization are maintained"

These references are informative and do not constitute compliance guidance.

## Notes

Detection guidance: Monitor for absence of hardware inventory systems, missing CMDB entries for physical devices, lack of asset tracking processes, or gaps in hardware asset documentation. Organizations should maintain comprehensive inventories that include physical devices, networking equipment, servers, workstations, mobile devices, and IoT devices.
