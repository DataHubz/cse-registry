# CSE-NISTCSF-AM-NO-ASSET-PRIORITIZATION-005

**Assets Not Prioritized Based on Criticality and Impact**

## Signal Overview

| Field           | Value                                    |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------|
| Identifier      | `CSE-NISTCSF-AM-NO-ASSET-PRIORITIZATION-005` |
| Domain          | NISTCSF                                  |
| Category        | AM                                       |
| Status          | Active                                   |
| Introduced In   | 1.0.0                                    |

## Description

Assets are not prioritized based on classification, criticality, resources, and mission impact.

## Applicability

This signal applies to:

- All organizations implementing cybersecurity risk management
- IT asset management and inventory teams
- Configuration management programs
- Organizations maintaining technology inventories

## NIST CSF 2.0 Context (Informative)

**Function:** IDENTIFY (ID)
**Category:** ID.AM - Asset Management
**Subcategory:** ID.AM-05

**Framework Text:** "Assets are prioritized based on classification, criticality, resources, and impact on the mission"

These references are informative and do not constitute compliance guidance.

## Notes

Detection guidance: Monitor for absence of asset classification schemes, missing criticality ratings, lack of business impact assessments, or gaps in asset prioritization frameworks. Organizations should implement risk-based prioritization that considers data sensitivity, business criticality, regulatory requirements, and operational dependencies.
