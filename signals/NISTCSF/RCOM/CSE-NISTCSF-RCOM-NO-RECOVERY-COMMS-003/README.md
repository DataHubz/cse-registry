# CSE-NISTCSF-RCOM-NO-RECOVERY-COMMS-003

**Recovery Activities and Progress Not Communicated to Stakeholders**

## Signal Overview

| Field           | Value                                    |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------|
| Identifier      | `CSE-NISTCSF-RCOM-NO-RECOVERY-COMMS-003` |
| Domain          | NISTCSF                                  |
| Category        | RCOM                                     |
| Status          | Active                                   |
| Introduced In   | 1.0.0                                    |

## Description

Recovery activities and progress are not communicated to stakeholders.

## Applicability

This signal applies to:

- All organizations with business continuity and disaster recovery requirements
- Incident recovery and restoration teams
- Communications and public relations functions
- Organizations with regulatory recovery requirements

## NIST CSF 2.0 Context (Informative)

**Function:** RECOVER (RC)
**Category:** RC.CO - Incident Recovery Communication
**Subcategory:** RC.CO-03

**Framework Text:** "Recovery activities and progress in restoring operational capabilities are communicated to designated internal and external stakeholders"

These references are informative and do not constitute compliance guidance.

## Notes

Detection guidance: Monitor for recovery operations conducted without stakeholder status updates, progress reports, or communication plans. Look for gaps in internal notifications to leadership, external updates to partners/customers, or regulatory reporting during recovery phases.
