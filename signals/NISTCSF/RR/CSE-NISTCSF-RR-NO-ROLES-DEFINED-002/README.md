# CSE-NISTCSF-RR-NO-ROLES-DEFINED-002

**Cybersecurity Roles and Responsibilities Not Defined**

## Signal Overview

| Field           | Value                                    |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------|
| Identifier      | `CSE-NISTCSF-RR-NO-ROLES-DEFINED-002` |
| Domain          | NISTCSF                                  |
| Category        | RR                                       |
| Status          | Active                                   |
| Introduced In   | 1.0.0                                    |

## Description

Roles, responsibilities, and authorities for cybersecurity are not established and communicated.

## Applicability

This signal applies to:

- All organizations implementing cybersecurity risk management
- Enterprise governance and leadership functions
- Strategic planning and risk management teams
- Organizations subject to regulatory compliance requirements

## NIST CSF 2.0 Context (Informative)

**Function:** GOVERN (GV)
**Category:** GV.RR - Roles, Responsibilities, and Authorities
**Subcategory:** GV.RR-02

**Framework Text:** "Roles, responsibilities, and authorities related to cybersecurity risk management are established, communicated, understood, and enforced"

These references are informative and do not constitute compliance guidance.

## Notes

Detection guidance: Identify organizations lacking documented cybersecurity roles and responsibilities, absence of RACI matrices or equivalent documentation, unclear authority delegation for security decisions, or evidence that personnel are unaware of their cybersecurity obligations.
