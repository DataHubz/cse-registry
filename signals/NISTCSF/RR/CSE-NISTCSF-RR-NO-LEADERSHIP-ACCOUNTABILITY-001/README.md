# CSE-NISTCSF-RR-NO-LEADERSHIP-ACCOUNTABILITY-001

**No Leadership Accountability for Cybersecurity Risk**

## Signal Overview

| Field           | Value                                    |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------|
| Identifier      | `CSE-NISTCSF-RR-NO-LEADERSHIP-ACCOUNTABILITY-001` |
| Domain          | NISTCSF                                  |
| Category        | RR                                       |
| Status          | Active                                   |
| Introduced In   | 1.0.0                                    |

## Description

Organizational leadership is not accountable for cybersecurity risk.

## Applicability

This signal applies to:

- All organizations implementing cybersecurity risk management
- Enterprise governance and leadership functions
- Strategic planning and risk management teams
- Organizations subject to regulatory compliance requirements

## NIST CSF 2.0 Context (Informative)

**Function:** GOVERN (GV)
**Category:** GV.RR - Roles, Responsibilities, and Authorities
**Subcategory:** GV.RR-01

**Framework Text:** "Organizational leadership is responsible and accountable for cybersecurity risk and fosters a culture that is risk-aware, ethical, and continually improving"

These references are informative and do not constitute compliance guidance.

## Notes

Detection guidance: Identify organizations where executive leadership has not formally accepted accountability for cybersecurity risk management, lacks documented cybersecurity responsibilities in role descriptions, or does not demonstrate active engagement in cybersecurity governance activities.
