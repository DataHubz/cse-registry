# CSE-NISTCSF-OV-NO-ADJUSTMENT-STRATEGY-002

**Cybersecurity Strategy Not Adjusted Based on Requirements**

## Signal Overview

| Field           | Value                                    |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------|
| Identifier      | `CSE-NISTCSF-OV-NO-ADJUSTMENT-STRATEGY-002` |
| Domain          | NISTCSF                                  |
| Category        | OV                                       |
| Status          | Active                                   |
| Introduced In   | 1.0.0                                    |

## Description

The cybersecurity risk management strategy is not adjusted based on outcomes.

## Applicability

This signal applies to:

- All organizations implementing cybersecurity risk management
- Enterprise governance and leadership functions
- Strategic planning and risk management teams
- Organizations subject to regulatory compliance requirements

## NIST CSF 2.0 Context (Informative)

**Function:** GOVERN (GV)
**Category:** GV.OV - Oversight
**Subcategory:** GV.OV-02

**Framework Text:** "The cybersecurity risk management strategy is reviewed and adjusted to ensure coverage of organizational requirements and risks"

These references are informative and do not constitute compliance guidance.

## Notes

Detection guidance: Identify organizations with static cybersecurity strategies that are not updated, lack of documented strategy revision processes, strategies not aligned with current organizational requirements or risk landscape, or absence of gap analysis to identify coverage deficiencies.
