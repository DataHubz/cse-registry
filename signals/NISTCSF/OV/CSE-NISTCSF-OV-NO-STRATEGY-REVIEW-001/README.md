# CSE-NISTCSF-OV-NO-STRATEGY-REVIEW-001

**Cybersecurity Strategy Outcomes Not Reviewed**

## Signal Overview

| Field           | Value                                    |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------|
| Identifier      | `CSE-NISTCSF-OV-NO-STRATEGY-REVIEW-001` |
| Domain          | NISTCSF                                  |
| Category        | OV                                       |
| Status          | Active                                   |
| Introduced In   | 1.0.0                                    |

## Description

Cybersecurity risk management strategy outcomes are not reviewed to inform improvements.

## Applicability

This signal applies to:

- All organizations implementing cybersecurity risk management
- Enterprise governance and leadership functions
- Strategic planning and risk management teams
- Organizations subject to regulatory compliance requirements

## NIST CSF 2.0 Context (Informative)

**Function:** GOVERN (GV)
**Category:** GV.OV - Oversight
**Subcategory:** GV.OV-01

**Framework Text:** "Cybersecurity risk management strategy outcomes are reviewed to inform and adjust strategy and direction"

These references are informative and do not constitute compliance guidance.

## Notes

Detection guidance: Identify organizations without regular strategy review processes, lack of metrics to measure strategy effectiveness, absence of governance forums to evaluate cybersecurity outcomes, or failure to use performance data to adjust strategic direction.
