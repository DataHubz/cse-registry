# CSE-NISTCSF-OV-NO-PERFORMANCE-EVALUATION-003

**Cybersecurity Performance Not Evaluated**

## Signal Overview

| Field           | Value                                    |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------|
| Identifier      | `CSE-NISTCSF-OV-NO-PERFORMANCE-EVALUATION-003` |
| Domain          | NISTCSF                                  |
| Category        | OV                                       |
| Status          | Active                                   |
| Introduced In   | 1.0.0                                    |

## Description

Organizational cybersecurity risk management performance is not evaluated and reviewed.

## Applicability

This signal applies to:

- All organizations implementing cybersecurity risk management
- Enterprise governance and leadership functions
- Strategic planning and risk management teams
- Organizations subject to regulatory compliance requirements

## NIST CSF 2.0 Context (Informative)

**Function:** GOVERN (GV)
**Category:** GV.OV - Oversight
**Subcategory:** GV.OV-03

**Framework Text:** "Organizational cybersecurity risk management performance is evaluated and reviewed for adjustments needed"

These references are informative and do not constitute compliance guidance.

## Notes

Detection guidance: Identify organizations without established cybersecurity performance metrics and KPIs, lack of regular performance reporting to leadership, absence of maturity assessments or capability evaluations, or failure to implement continuous improvement based on performance reviews.
