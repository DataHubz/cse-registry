# CSE-NISTCSF-RM-NO-STRATEGIC-OPPORTUNITIES-007

**No Strategic Opportunities in Risk Discussions**

## Signal Overview

| Field           | Value                                    |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------|
| Identifier      | `CSE-NISTCSF-RM-NO-STRATEGIC-OPPORTUNITIES-007` |
| Domain          | NISTCSF                                  |
| Category        | RM                                       |
| Status          | Active                                   |
| Introduced In   | 1.0.0                                    |

## Description

Strategic opportunities are not characterized and included in risk discussions.

## Applicability

This signal applies to:

- All organizations implementing cybersecurity risk management
- Enterprise governance and leadership functions
- Strategic planning and risk management teams
- Organizations subject to regulatory compliance requirements

## NIST CSF 2.0 Context (Informative)

**Function:** GOVERN (GV)
**Category:** GV.RM - Risk Management Strategy
**Subcategory:** GV.RM-07

**Framework Text:** "Strategic opportunities (i.e., positive risks) are characterized and are included in organizational cybersecurity risk discussions"

These references are informative and do not constitute compliance guidance.

## Notes

Detection guidance: Look for risk discussions focused only on negative outcomes, absence of opportunity identification in risk assessments, missing consideration of competitive advantages from security investments, lack of positive risk evaluation frameworks.
