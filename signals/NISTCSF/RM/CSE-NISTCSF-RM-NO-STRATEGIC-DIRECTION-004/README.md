# CSE-NISTCSF-RM-NO-STRATEGIC-DIRECTION-004

**No Risk-Informed Strategic Direction**

## Signal Overview

| Field           | Value                                    |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------|
| Identifier      | `CSE-NISTCSF-RM-NO-STRATEGIC-DIRECTION-004` |
| Domain          | NISTCSF                                  |
| Category        | RM                                       |
| Status          | Active                                   |
| Introduced In   | 1.0.0                                    |

## Description

Strategic direction is not informed by risk management outcomes.

## Applicability

This signal applies to:

- All organizations implementing cybersecurity risk management
- Enterprise governance and leadership functions
- Strategic planning and risk management teams
- Organizations subject to regulatory compliance requirements

## NIST CSF 2.0 Context (Informative)

**Function:** GOVERN (GV)
**Category:** GV.RM - Risk Management Strategy
**Subcategory:** GV.RM-04

**Framework Text:** "Strategic direction that describes appropriate risk response options is established and communicated"

These references are informative and do not constitute compliance guidance.

## Notes

Detection guidance: Look for strategic plans without risk considerations, missing risk response options, lack of documented risk treatment decisions, absence of risk-based prioritization in strategic initiatives.
