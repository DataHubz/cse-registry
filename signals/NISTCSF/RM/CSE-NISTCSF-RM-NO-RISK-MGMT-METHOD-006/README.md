# CSE-NISTCSF-RM-NO-RISK-MGMT-METHOD-006

**No Standardized Risk Management Method**

## Signal Overview

| Field           | Value                                    |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------|
| Identifier      | `CSE-NISTCSF-RM-NO-RISK-MGMT-METHOD-006` |
| Domain          | NISTCSF                                  |
| Category        | RM                                       |
| Status          | Active                                   |
| Introduced In   | 1.0.0                                    |

## Description

A standardized method for calculating, documenting, and tracking risks is not established.

## Applicability

This signal applies to:

- All organizations implementing cybersecurity risk management
- Enterprise governance and leadership functions
- Strategic planning and risk management teams
- Organizations subject to regulatory compliance requirements

## NIST CSF 2.0 Context (Informative)

**Function:** GOVERN (GV)
**Category:** GV.RM - Risk Management Strategy
**Subcategory:** GV.RM-06

**Framework Text:** "A standardized method for calculating, documenting, categorizing, and prioritizing cybersecurity risks is established and communicated"

These references are informative and do not constitute compliance guidance.

## Notes

Detection guidance: Look for inconsistent risk calculation methodologies, absence of risk scoring frameworks, missing risk categorization schemas, lack of standardized risk documentation templates, undefined prioritization criteria.
