# CSE-NISTCSF-RM-NO-SUPPLY-CHAIN-LINES-005

**No Supply Chain Risk Communication Lines**

## Signal Overview

| Field           | Value                                    |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------|
| Identifier      | `CSE-NISTCSF-RM-NO-SUPPLY-CHAIN-LINES-005` |
| Domain          | NISTCSF                                  |
| Category        | RM                                       |
| Status          | Active                                   |
| Introduced In   | 1.0.0                                    |

## Description

Lines of communication for cybersecurity risks across the supply chain are not established.

## Applicability

This signal applies to:

- All organizations implementing cybersecurity risk management
- Enterprise governance and leadership functions
- Strategic planning and risk management teams
- Organizations subject to regulatory compliance requirements

## NIST CSF 2.0 Context (Informative)

**Function:** GOVERN (GV)
**Category:** GV.RM - Risk Management Strategy
**Subcategory:** GV.RM-05

**Framework Text:** "Lines of communication across the organization are established for cybersecurity risks, including risks from suppliers and other third parties"

These references are informative and do not constitute compliance guidance.

## Notes

Detection guidance: Look for missing vendor risk communication processes, absence of third-party risk escalation paths, lack of supply chain risk reporting mechanisms, undefined communication channels for supplier security incidents.
