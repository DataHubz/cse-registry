# CSE-NISTCSF-RM-NO-RISK-MGMT-OBJECTIVES-001

**No Risk Management Objectives**

## Signal Overview

| Field           | Value                                    |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------|
| Identifier      | `CSE-NISTCSF-RM-NO-RISK-MGMT-OBJECTIVES-001` |
| Domain          | NISTCSF                                  |
| Category        | RM                                       |
| Status          | Active                                   |
| Introduced In   | 1.0.0                                    |

## Description

Risk management objectives are not established and agreed to by organizational stakeholders.

## Applicability

This signal applies to:

- All organizations implementing cybersecurity risk management
- Enterprise governance and leadership functions
- Strategic planning and risk management teams
- Organizations subject to regulatory compliance requirements

## NIST CSF 2.0 Context (Informative)

**Function:** GOVERN (GV)
**Category:** GV.RM - Risk Management Strategy
**Subcategory:** GV.RM-01

**Framework Text:** "Risk management objectives are established and agreed to by organizational stakeholders"

These references are informative and do not constitute compliance guidance.

## Notes

Detection guidance: Look for absence of documented risk management objectives, missing stakeholder agreement on risk priorities, lack of formal risk appetite statements.
