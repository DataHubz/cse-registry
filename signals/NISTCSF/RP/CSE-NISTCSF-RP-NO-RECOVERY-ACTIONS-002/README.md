# CSE-NISTCSF-RP-NO-RECOVERY-ACTIONS-002

**Recovery Actions Not Selected, Scoped, Prioritized, and Performed**

## Signal Overview

| Field           | Value                                    |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------|
| Identifier      | `CSE-NISTCSF-RP-NO-RECOVERY-ACTIONS-002` |
| Domain          | NISTCSF                                  |
| Category        | RP                                       |
| Status          | Active                                   |
| Introduced In   | 1.0.0                                    |

## Description

Recovery actions are not selected, scoped, prioritized, and performed.

## Applicability

This signal applies to:

- All organizations with business continuity and disaster recovery requirements
- Incident recovery and restoration teams
- Communications and public relations functions
- Organizations with regulatory recovery requirements

## NIST CSF 2.0 Context (Informative)

**Function:** RECOVER (RC)
**Category:** RC.RP - Incident Recovery Plan Execution
**Subcategory:** RC.RP-02

**Framework Text:** "Recovery actions are selected, scoped, prioritized, and performed"

These references are informative and do not constitute compliance guidance.

## Notes

Detection guidance: Identify recovery efforts that lack documented selection criteria, scope definition, or prioritization frameworks. Monitor for random or reactive recovery actions without clear business impact analysis or sequencing based on critical function dependencies.
