# CSE-NISTCSF-CO-NO-INFO-SHARING-003

**Information Not Shared with Designated Stakeholders**

## Signal Overview

| Field           | Value                                    |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------|
| Identifier      | `CSE-NISTCSF-CO-NO-INFO-SHARING-003` |
| Domain          | NISTCSF                                  |
| Category        | RS.CO                                    |
| Status          | Active                                   |
| Introduced In   | 1.0.0                                    |

## Description

Information is not shared with designated internal and external stakeholders.

## Applicability

This signal applies to:

- All organizations with incident response capabilities
- Security operations and incident response teams
- Forensics and investigation functions
- Organizations with breach notification requirements

## NIST CSF 2.0 Context (Informative)

**Function:** RESPOND (RS)
**Category:** RS.CO - Incident Response Reporting and Communication
**Subcategory:** RS.CO-03

**Framework Text:** "Information is shared with designated internal and external stakeholders"

These references are informative and do not constitute compliance guidance.

## Notes

Detection guidance: Look for incidents where threat intelligence, indicators of compromise, or lessons learned were not shared with ISACs, peer organizations, or internal security teams. Monitor for missing contributions to threat sharing platforms, lack of post-incident reports to stakeholders, or incidents not communicated to affected business units or service providers who could benefit from the information.
