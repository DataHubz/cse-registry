# CSE-NISTCSF-CO-NO-STAKEHOLDER-NOTIFICATION-002

**Internal and External Stakeholders Not Notified of Incidents**

## Signal Overview

| Field           | Value                                    |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------|
| Identifier      | `CSE-NISTCSF-CO-NO-STAKEHOLDER-NOTIFICATION-002` |
| Domain          | NISTCSF                                  |
| Category        | RS.CO                                    |
| Status          | Active                                   |
| Introduced In   | 1.0.0                                    |

## Description

Internal and external stakeholders are not notified of incidents.

## Applicability

This signal applies to:

- All organizations with incident response capabilities
- Security operations and incident response teams
- Forensics and investigation functions
- Organizations with breach notification requirements

## NIST CSF 2.0 Context (Informative)

**Function:** RESPOND (RS)
**Category:** RS.CO - Incident Response Reporting and Communication
**Subcategory:** RS.CO-02

**Framework Text:** "Internal and external stakeholders are notified of incidents"

These references are informative and do not constitute compliance guidance.

## Notes

Detection guidance: Monitor for incidents affecting stakeholders without corresponding notification records, missing breach notifications to regulatory bodies within required timeframes, lack of customer communications for data breaches, or internal incidents not reported to executive management or board. Check for gaps in notification logs, missed SLA requirements for stakeholder updates, or incidents closed without stakeholder notification documentation.
