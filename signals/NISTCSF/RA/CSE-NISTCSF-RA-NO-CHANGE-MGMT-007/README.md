# CSE-NISTCSF-RA-NO-CHANGE-MGMT-007

**No Change Management Risk Assessment**

## Signal Overview

| Field           | Value                                    |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------|
| Identifier      | `CSE-NISTCSF-RA-NO-CHANGE-MGMT-007` |
| Domain          | NISTCSF                                  |
| Category        | RA                                       |
| Status          | Active                                   |
| Introduced In   | 1.0.0                                    |

## Description

Changes and exceptions are not managed, assessed for risk impact, and tracked.

## Applicability

This signal applies to:

- All organizations implementing cybersecurity risk management
- Risk assessment and vulnerability management teams
- Security operations and threat intelligence functions
- Organizations conducting security assessments

## NIST CSF 2.0 Context (Informative)

**Function:** IDENTIFY (ID)
**Category:** ID.RA - Risk Assessment
**Subcategory:** ID.RA-07

**Framework Text:** "Changes and exceptions are managed, assessed for risk impact, recorded, and tracked"

These references are informative and do not constitute compliance guidance.

## Notes

Detection guidance: This signal indicates that change management processes do not include risk assessment steps, or that policy exceptions and deviations are not evaluated for risk impact. Organizations should integrate risk assessment into change management workflows and establish exception tracking and review processes.
