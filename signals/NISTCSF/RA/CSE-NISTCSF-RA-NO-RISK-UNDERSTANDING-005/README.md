# CSE-NISTCSF-RA-NO-RISK-UNDERSTANDING-005

**No Risk Understanding**

## Signal Overview

| Field           | Value                                    |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------|
| Identifier      | `CSE-NISTCSF-RA-NO-RISK-UNDERSTANDING-005` |
| Domain          | NISTCSF                                  |
| Category        | RA                                       |
| Status          | Active                                   |
| Introduced In   | 1.0.0                                    |

## Description

Threats, vulnerabilities, likelihoods, and impacts are not used to understand inherent risk.

## Applicability

This signal applies to:

- All organizations implementing cybersecurity risk management
- Risk assessment and vulnerability management teams
- Security operations and threat intelligence functions
- Organizations conducting security assessments

## NIST CSF 2.0 Context (Informative)

**Function:** IDENTIFY (ID)
**Category:** ID.RA - Risk Assessment
**Subcategory:** ID.RA-05

**Framework Text:** "Threats, vulnerabilities, likelihoods, and impacts are used to understand inherent risk and inform risk response prioritization"

These references are informative and do not constitute compliance guidance.

## Notes

Detection guidance: This signal indicates that the organization is not synthesizing threat, vulnerability, and impact data to calculate inherent risk or prioritize risk responses. Organizations should implement risk calculation and prioritization frameworks that combine these elements to inform decision-making and resource allocation.
