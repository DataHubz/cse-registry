# CSE-NISTCSF-RA-NO-THREAT-INTEL-002

**No Threat Intelligence**

## Signal Overview

| Field           | Value                                    |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------|
| Identifier      | `CSE-NISTCSF-RA-NO-THREAT-INTEL-002` |
| Domain          | NISTCSF                                  |
| Category        | RA                                       |
| Status          | Active                                   |
| Introduced In   | 1.0.0                                    |

## Description

Cyber threat intelligence is not received from information sharing forums and sources.

## Applicability

This signal applies to:

- All organizations implementing cybersecurity risk management
- Risk assessment and vulnerability management teams
- Security operations and threat intelligence functions
- Organizations conducting security assessments

## NIST CSF 2.0 Context (Informative)

**Function:** IDENTIFY (ID)
**Category:** ID.RA - Risk Assessment
**Subcategory:** ID.RA-02

**Framework Text:** "Cyber threat intelligence is received from information sharing forums and sources"

These references are informative and do not constitute compliance guidance.

## Notes

Detection guidance: This signal indicates that the organization is not participating in or receiving threat intelligence from industry information sharing and analysis centers (ISACs), threat intelligence platforms, or other threat data sources. Organizations should establish relationships with relevant threat intelligence communities and implement processes to consume and act on threat intelligence.
