# CSE-NISTCSF-SC-NO-SCRM-ROLES-002

**No Cybersecurity Roles and Responsibilities for Suppliers**

## Signal Overview

| Field           | Value                                    |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------|
| Identifier      | `CSE-NISTCSF-SC-NO-SCRM-ROLES-002` |
| Domain          | NISTCSF                                  |
| Category        | SC                                       |
| Status          | Active                                   |
| Introduced In   | 1.0.0                                    |

## Description

Cybersecurity roles and responsibilities for suppliers are not established.

## Applicability

This signal applies to:

- All organizations implementing cybersecurity risk management
- Organizations with supplier and third-party relationships
- Procurement and vendor management teams
- Supply chain security programs

## NIST CSF 2.0 Context (Informative)

**Function:** GOVERN (GV)
**Category:** GV.SC - Cybersecurity Supply Chain Risk Management
**Subcategory:** GV.SC-02

**Framework Text:** "Cybersecurity roles and responsibilities for suppliers, customers, and partners are established, communicated, and coordinated internally and externally"

These references are informative and do not constitute compliance guidance.

## Notes

Detection guidance: Look for the absence of defined cybersecurity roles and responsibilities for suppliers, customers, and partners. Check for lack of communication or coordination mechanisms for these roles across internal and external stakeholders.
