# CSE-NISTCSF-SC-NO-SCRM-INTEGRATION-003

**No Integration of Supply Chain Risk Management into Cybersecurity and Enterprise Risk Management**

## Signal Overview

| Field           | Value                                    |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------|
| Identifier      | `CSE-NISTCSF-SC-NO-SCRM-INTEGRATION-003` |
| Domain          | NISTCSF                                  |
| Category        | SC                                       |
| Status          | Active                                   |
| Introduced In   | 1.0.0                                    |

## Description

Cybersecurity supply chain risk management is not integrated into cybersecurity and enterprise risk management.

## Applicability

This signal applies to:

- All organizations implementing cybersecurity risk management
- Organizations with supplier and third-party relationships
- Procurement and vendor management teams
- Supply chain security programs

## NIST CSF 2.0 Context (Informative)

**Function:** GOVERN (GV)
**Category:** GV.SC - Cybersecurity Supply Chain Risk Management
**Subcategory:** GV.SC-03

**Framework Text:** "Cybersecurity supply chain risk management is integrated into cybersecurity and enterprise risk management, risk assessment, and improvement processes"

These references are informative and do not constitute compliance guidance.

## Notes

Detection guidance: Look for the absence of integration between supply chain risk management and broader cybersecurity or enterprise risk management programs. Check for lack of inclusion in risk assessment processes and continuous improvement activities.
