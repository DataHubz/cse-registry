# CSE-NISTCSF-SC-NO-SCRM-PRACTICES-009

**Supply Chain Security Practices Not Integrated into Acquisitions**

## Signal Overview

| Field           | Value                                    |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------|
| Identifier      | `CSE-NISTCSF-SC-NO-SCRM-PRACTICES-009` |
| Domain          | NISTCSF                                  |
| Category        | SC                                       |
| Status          | Active                                   |
| Introduced In   | 1.0.0                                    |

## Description

Supply chain security practices are not integrated into acquisitions.

## Applicability

This signal applies to:

- All organizations implementing cybersecurity risk management
- Organizations with supplier and third-party relationships
- Procurement and vendor management teams
- Supply chain security programs

## NIST CSF 2.0 Context (Informative)

**Function:** GOVERN (GV)
**Category:** GV.SC - Cybersecurity Supply Chain Risk Management
**Subcategory:** GV.SC-09

**Framework Text:** "Supply chain security practices are integrated into cybersecurity and enterprise risk management programs, and their performance is monitored throughout the technology product and service life cycle"

These references are informative and do not constitute compliance guidance.

## Notes

Detection guidance: Look for the absence of supply chain security practices in acquisition processes and lifecycle management. Check for lack of performance monitoring of security practices throughout the technology product and service lifecycle.
