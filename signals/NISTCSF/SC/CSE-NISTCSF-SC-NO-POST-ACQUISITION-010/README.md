# CSE-NISTCSF-SC-NO-POST-ACQUISITION-010

**No Post-Acquisition Supply Chain Risk Management Plans**

## Signal Overview

| Field           | Value                                    |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------|
| Identifier      | `CSE-NISTCSF-SC-NO-POST-ACQUISITION-010` |
| Domain          | NISTCSF                                  |
| Category        | SC                                       |
| Status          | Active                                   |
| Introduced In   | 1.0.0                                    |

## Description

Cybersecurity supply chain risk management plans are not monitored post-acquisition.

## Applicability

This signal applies to:

- All organizations implementing cybersecurity risk management
- Organizations with supplier and third-party relationships
- Procurement and vendor management teams
- Supply chain security programs

## NIST CSF 2.0 Context (Informative)

**Function:** GOVERN (GV)
**Category:** GV.SC - Cybersecurity Supply Chain Risk Management
**Subcategory:** GV.SC-10

**Framework Text:** "Cybersecurity supply chain risk management plans include provisions for activities that occur after the conclusion of a partnership or service agreement"

These references are informative and do not constitute compliance guidance.

## Notes

Detection guidance: Look for the absence of provisions in SCRM plans for post-contract or post-partnership activities. Check for lack of processes to handle data return, asset recovery, or ongoing obligations after supplier relationships end.
