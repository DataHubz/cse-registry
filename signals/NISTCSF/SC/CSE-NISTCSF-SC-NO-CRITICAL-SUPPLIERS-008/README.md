# CSE-NISTCSF-SC-NO-CRITICAL-SUPPLIERS-008

**Suppliers Not Included in Incident Planning and Response**

## Signal Overview

| Field           | Value                                    |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------|
| Identifier      | `CSE-NISTCSF-SC-NO-CRITICAL-SUPPLIERS-008` |
| Domain          | NISTCSF                                  |
| Category        | SC                                       |
| Status          | Active                                   |
| Introduced In   | 1.0.0                                    |

## Description

Relevant suppliers and third parties are not included in cybersecurity exercises.

## Applicability

This signal applies to:

- All organizations implementing cybersecurity risk management
- Organizations with supplier and third-party relationships
- Procurement and vendor management teams
- Supply chain security programs

## NIST CSF 2.0 Context (Informative)

**Function:** GOVERN (GV)
**Category:** GV.SC - Cybersecurity Supply Chain Risk Management
**Subcategory:** GV.SC-08

**Framework Text:** "Relevant suppliers and other third parties are included in incident planning, response, and recovery activities"

These references are informative and do not constitute compliance guidance.

## Notes

Detection guidance: Look for the absence of supplier involvement in incident planning, response exercises, and recovery activities. Check for lack of coordination mechanisms or communication channels with critical suppliers during incident scenarios.
