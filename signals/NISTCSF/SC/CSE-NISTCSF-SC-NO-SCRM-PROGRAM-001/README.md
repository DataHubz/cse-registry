# CSE-NISTCSF-SC-NO-SCRM-PROGRAM-001

**No Cybersecurity Supply Chain Risk Management Program**

## Signal Overview

| Field           | Value                                    |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------|
| Identifier      | `CSE-NISTCSF-SC-NO-SCRM-PROGRAM-001` |
| Domain          | NISTCSF                                  |
| Category        | SC                                       |
| Status          | Active                                   |
| Introduced In   | 1.0.0                                    |

## Description

A cybersecurity supply chain risk management program and strategy are not established.

## Applicability

This signal applies to:

- All organizations implementing cybersecurity risk management
- Organizations with supplier and third-party relationships
- Procurement and vendor management teams
- Supply chain security programs

## NIST CSF 2.0 Context (Informative)

**Function:** GOVERN (GV)
**Category:** GV.SC - Cybersecurity Supply Chain Risk Management
**Subcategory:** GV.SC-01

**Framework Text:** "A cybersecurity supply chain risk management program, strategy, objectives, policies, and processes are established and agreed to by organizational stakeholders"

These references are informative and do not constitute compliance guidance.

## Notes

Detection guidance: Look for the absence of documented supply chain risk management programs, strategies, objectives, policies, or processes. Check for lack of stakeholder agreement or formal approval of SCRM initiatives.
