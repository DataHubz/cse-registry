# CSE-NISTCSF-OC-NO-LEGAL-REQUIREMENTS-003

**No Legal Requirements Management**

## Signal Overview

| Field           | Value                                    |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------|
| Identifier      | `CSE-NISTCSF-OC-NO-LEGAL-REQUIREMENTS-003` |
| Domain          | NISTCSF                                  |
| Category        | OC                                       |
| Status          | Active                                   |
| Introduced In   | 1.0.0                                    |

## Description

Legal, regulatory, and contractual cybersecurity requirements are not understood and managed.

## Applicability

This signal applies to:

- All organizations implementing cybersecurity risk management
- Enterprise governance and leadership functions
- Strategic planning and risk management teams
- Organizations subject to regulatory compliance requirements

## NIST CSF 2.0 Context (Informative)

**Function:** GOVERN (GV)
**Category:** GV.OC - Organizational Context
**Subcategory:** GV.OC-03

**Framework Text:** "Legal, regulatory, and contractual requirements regarding cybersecurity — including privacy and civil liberties obligations — are understood and managed"

These references are informative and do not constitute compliance guidance.

## Notes

Detection guidance: Look for absence of legal and regulatory requirement inventories, missing compliance tracking mechanisms, lack of documented privacy and civil liberties obligations, no evidence of contractual cybersecurity requirement management, absence of legal counsel involvement in cybersecurity governance.
