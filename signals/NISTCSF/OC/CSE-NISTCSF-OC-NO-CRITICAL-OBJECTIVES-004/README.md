# CSE-NISTCSF-OC-NO-CRITICAL-OBJECTIVES-004

**No Critical Objectives for External Stakeholders**

## Signal Overview

| Field           | Value                                    |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------|
| Identifier      | `CSE-NISTCSF-OC-NO-CRITICAL-OBJECTIVES-004` |
| Domain          | NISTCSF                                  |
| Category        | OC                                       |
| Status          | Active                                   |
| Introduced In   | 1.0.0                                    |

## Description

Critical objectives, capabilities, and services for stakeholders have not been determined.

## Applicability

This signal applies to:

- All organizations implementing cybersecurity risk management
- Enterprise governance and leadership functions
- Strategic planning and risk management teams
- Organizations subject to regulatory compliance requirements

## NIST CSF 2.0 Context (Informative)

**Function:** GOVERN (GV)
**Category:** GV.OC - Organizational Context
**Subcategory:** GV.OC-04

**Framework Text:** "Critical objectives, capabilities, and services that external stakeholders depend on or expect from the organization are understood and communicated"

These references are informative and do not constitute compliance guidance.

## Notes

Detection guidance: Look for absence of critical service catalogs, missing documentation of external stakeholder dependencies, lack of service level agreements or commitments, no evidence of external stakeholder expectation management, missing business impact analysis identifying critical capabilities.
