# CSE-NISTCSF-OC-NO-STAKEHOLDER-COMMS-002

**No Internal Stakeholder Communications**

## Signal Overview

| Field           | Value                                    |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------|
| Identifier      | `CSE-NISTCSF-OC-NO-STAKEHOLDER-COMMS-002` |
| Domain          | NISTCSF                                  |
| Category        | OC                                       |
| Status          | Active                                   |
| Introduced In   | 1.0.0                                    |

## Description

Internal stakeholders are not identified and their expectations not communicated.

## Applicability

This signal applies to:

- All organizations implementing cybersecurity risk management
- Enterprise governance and leadership functions
- Strategic planning and risk management teams
- Organizations subject to regulatory compliance requirements

## NIST CSF 2.0 Context (Informative)

**Function:** GOVERN (GV)
**Category:** GV.OC - Organizational Context
**Subcategory:** GV.OC-02

**Framework Text:** "Internal stakeholders are identified, and their expectations and needs are understood and communicated"

These references are informative and do not constitute compliance guidance.

## Notes

Detection guidance: Look for absence of stakeholder identification documents, missing stakeholder needs assessments, lack of documented communication channels with internal stakeholders regarding cybersecurity expectations, no evidence of stakeholder input in security planning processes.
