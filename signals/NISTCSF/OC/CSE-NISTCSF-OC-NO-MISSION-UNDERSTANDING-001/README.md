# CSE-NISTCSF-OC-NO-MISSION-UNDERSTANDING-001

**No Organizational Mission Understanding**

## Signal Overview

| Field           | Value                                    |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------|
| Identifier      | `CSE-NISTCSF-OC-NO-MISSION-UNDERSTANDING-001` |
| Domain          | NISTCSF                                  |
| Category        | OC                                       |
| Status          | Active                                   |
| Introduced In   | 1.0.0                                    |

## Description

The organizational mission is not understood to inform cybersecurity risk management.

## Applicability

This signal applies to:

- All organizations implementing cybersecurity risk management
- Enterprise governance and leadership functions
- Strategic planning and risk management teams
- Organizations subject to regulatory compliance requirements

## NIST CSF 2.0 Context (Informative)

**Function:** GOVERN (GV)
**Category:** GV.OC - Organizational Context
**Subcategory:** GV.OC-01

**Framework Text:** "The organizational mission is understood and informs cybersecurity risk management"

These references are informative and do not constitute compliance guidance.

## Notes

Detection guidance: Look for absence of documented organizational mission statements that connect to cybersecurity strategy, missing alignment between business objectives and security priorities, lack of documented understanding of how cybersecurity supports organizational goals.
