# CSE-NISTCSF-OC-NO-OUTCOMES-PRIORITIZED-005

**No Outcomes Prioritized Based on Dependencies**

## Signal Overview

| Field           | Value                                    |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------|
| Identifier      | `CSE-NISTCSF-OC-NO-OUTCOMES-PRIORITIZED-005` |
| Domain          | NISTCSF                                  |
| Category        | OC                                       |
| Status          | Active                                   |
| Introduced In   | 1.0.0                                    |

## Description

Outcomes, capabilities, and services that depend on others are not prioritized.

## Applicability

This signal applies to:

- All organizations implementing cybersecurity risk management
- Enterprise governance and leadership functions
- Strategic planning and risk management teams
- Organizations subject to regulatory compliance requirements

## NIST CSF 2.0 Context (Informative)

**Function:** GOVERN (GV)
**Category:** GV.OC - Organizational Context
**Subcategory:** GV.OC-05

**Framework Text:** "Outcomes, capabilities, and services that the organization depends on are understood and communicated"

These references are informative and do not constitute compliance guidance.

## Notes

Detection guidance: Look for absence of dependency mapping documentation, missing third-party and vendor risk assessments, lack of documented critical dependencies on external services, no evidence of supply chain risk management, missing documentation of organizational reliance on external capabilities.
