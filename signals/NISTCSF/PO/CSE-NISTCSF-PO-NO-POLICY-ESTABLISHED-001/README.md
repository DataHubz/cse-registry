# CSE-NISTCSF-PO-NO-POLICY-ESTABLISHED-001

**Cybersecurity Risk Management Policy Not Established**

## Signal Overview

| Field           | Value                                    |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------|
| Identifier      | `CSE-NISTCSF-PO-NO-POLICY-ESTABLISHED-001` |
| Domain          | NISTCSF                                  |
| Category        | PO                                       |
| Status          | Active                                   |
| Introduced In   | 1.0.0                                    |

## Description

A policy for cybersecurity risk management based on organizational context is not established.

## Applicability

This signal applies to:

- All organizations implementing cybersecurity risk management
- Enterprise governance and leadership functions
- Strategic planning and risk management teams
- Organizations subject to regulatory compliance requirements

## NIST CSF 2.0 Context (Informative)

**Function:** GOVERN (GV)
**Category:** GV.PO - Policy
**Subcategory:** GV.PO-01

**Framework Text:** "Policy for managing cybersecurity risks is established based on organizational context, cybersecurity strategy, and priorities and is communicated and enforced"

These references are informative and do not constitute compliance guidance.

## Notes

Detection guidance: Identify organizations lacking documented cybersecurity policies, policies not aligned with organizational context and risk appetite, absence of formal policy approval and communication processes, or lack of enforcement mechanisms for policy compliance.
