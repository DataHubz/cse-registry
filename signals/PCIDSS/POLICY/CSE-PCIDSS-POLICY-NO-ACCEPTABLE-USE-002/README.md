# CSE-PCIDSS-POLICY-NO-ACCEPTABLE-USE-002

**No Acceptable Use Policy**

## Signal Overview

| Field           | Value                                    |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------|
| Identifier      | `CSE-PCIDSS-POLICY-NO-ACCEPTABLE-USE-002` |
| Domain          | PCIDSS                                   |
| Category        | POLICY                                   |
| Status          | Active                                   |
| Introduced In   | 1.0.0                                    |

## Description

No acceptable use policies for critical technologies.

## Applicability

This signal applies to:

- Organizations processing, storing, or transmitting cardholder data
- Security and compliance teams
- Human resources and training departments
- Third-party service providers (TPSPs)
- Incident response teams

## PCI DSS Context (Informative)

**Requirement:** 12.2 - Acceptable use policies for end-user technologies are defined and implemented

**Sub-Requirement:** 12.2.1

**Regulatory Text:** "Acceptable use policies for end-user technologies are documented and implemented, including: Explicit management approval, acceptable uses of the technology, and list of products approved by the company for employee use, including hardware and software."

These references are informative and do not constitute compliance guidance.

## Notes

Detection guidance: Verify existence of acceptable use policies covering end-user technologies such as remote access, wireless, removable media, laptops, tablets, handheld devices, email, and internet usage. Monitor for employee acknowledgment and periodic review of policies.
