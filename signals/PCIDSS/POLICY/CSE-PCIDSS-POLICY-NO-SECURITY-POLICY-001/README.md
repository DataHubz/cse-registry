# CSE-PCIDSS-POLICY-NO-SECURITY-POLICY-001

**No Information Security Policy**

## Signal Overview

| Field           | Value                                    |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------|
| Identifier      | `CSE-PCIDSS-POLICY-NO-SECURITY-POLICY-001` |
| Domain          | PCIDSS                                   |
| Category        | POLICY                                   |
| Status          | Active                                   |
| Introduced In   | 1.0.0                                    |

## Description

No comprehensive information security policy maintained and kept current.

## Applicability

This signal applies to:

- Organizations processing, storing, or transmitting cardholder data
- Security and compliance teams
- Human resources and training departments
- Third-party service providers (TPSPs)
- Incident response teams

## PCI DSS Context (Informative)

**Requirement:** 12.1 - A comprehensive information security policy that governs and provides direction for protection of the entity's information assets is known and current

**Sub-Requirement:** 12.1.1

**Regulatory Text:** "An overall information security policy is: Established, published, maintained, and disseminated to all relevant personnel, including vendors and business partners, reviewed at least once every 12 months, and updated as needed based on the results of the reviews."

These references are informative and do not constitute compliance guidance.

## Notes

Detection guidance: Monitor for the absence of a documented, board-approved information security policy. Verify that the policy addresses all PCI DSS requirements, is reviewed annually, and is distributed to all affected personnel. Check for policy version control and acknowledgment tracking.
