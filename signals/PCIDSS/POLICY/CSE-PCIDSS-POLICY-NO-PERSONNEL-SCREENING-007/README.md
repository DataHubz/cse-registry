# CSE-PCIDSS-POLICY-NO-PERSONNEL-SCREENING-007

**No Personnel Screening**

## Signal Overview

| Field           | Value                                    |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------|
| Identifier      | `CSE-PCIDSS-POLICY-NO-PERSONNEL-SCREENING-007` |
| Domain          | PCIDSS                                   |
| Category        | POLICY                                   |
| Status          | Active                                   |
| Introduced In   | 1.0.0                                    |

## Description

Personnel are not screened to minimize insider threat risk.

## Applicability

This signal applies to:

- Organizations processing, storing, or transmitting cardholder data
- Security and compliance teams
- Human resources and training departments
- Third-party service providers (TPSPs)
- Incident response teams

## PCI DSS Context (Informative)

**Requirement:** 12.7 - Personnel are screened to reduce risks from insider threats

**Sub-Requirement:** 12.7.1

**Regulatory Text:** "Potential personnel who will have access to the cardholder data environment are screened, within the constraints of local laws, prior to hire to minimize the risk of attacks from internal sources."

These references are informative and do not constitute compliance guidance.

## Notes

Detection guidance: Verify existence of pre-employment screening procedures for personnel with CDE access, including background checks, reference checks, and identity verification. Monitor HR records for screening completion and compliance with local laws. Check for screening of contractors and third-party personnel.
