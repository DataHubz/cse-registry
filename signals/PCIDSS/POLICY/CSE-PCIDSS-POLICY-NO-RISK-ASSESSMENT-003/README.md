# CSE-PCIDSS-POLICY-NO-RISK-ASSESSMENT-003

**No Risk Assessment**

## Signal Overview

| Field           | Value                                    |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------|
| Identifier      | `CSE-PCIDSS-POLICY-NO-RISK-ASSESSMENT-003` |
| Domain          | PCIDSS                                   |
| Category        | POLICY                                   |
| Status          | Active                                   |
| Introduced In   | 1.0.0                                    |

## Description

Risks to the CDE are not formally identified, evaluated, and managed.

## Applicability

This signal applies to:

- Organizations processing, storing, or transmitting cardholder data
- Security and compliance teams
- Human resources and training departments
- Third-party service providers (TPSPs)
- Incident response teams

## PCI DSS Context (Informative)

**Requirement:** 12.3 - Risks to the cardholder data environment are formally identified, evaluated, and managed

**Sub-Requirement:** 12.3.1

**Regulatory Text:** "Targeted risk analyses are performed to identify threats and vulnerabilities, and the results are documented. At a minimum, risk assessments are performed: Upon significant changes to the environment, and at least once every 12 months."

These references are informative and do not constitute compliance guidance.

## Notes

Detection guidance: Check for documented risk assessment methodology and recent risk assessment reports covering the CDE. Verify annual risk assessments and assessments after significant changes. Monitor for risk treatment plans and evidence of risk-based decision making.
